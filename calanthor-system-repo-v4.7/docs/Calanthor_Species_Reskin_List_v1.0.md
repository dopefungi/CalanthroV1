# Calanthor Species & Reskin List (v1.0)
*Instantly recognizable 5e silhouettes, Calanthor names, and Spirit Alignment “typical dominions.”*

**Important rule (always applies):** **Born Under the Pet overrides everything.**
- If you are born in a **Dark Pet** dominion, you are **Dark-aligned**, regardless of species.
- If you are born in a **Light Pet** dominion, you are **Light-aligned**, regardless of species.

The “Typical Spirit” notes below describe the most common birth dominions *for that clade*, not a restriction.

---

## 0) Great Pets quick key
**Light Pets:** Gaianok (Verdant), Shakora (Waters), Keairvari (Skyward Peaks), Nyvakira (Emberwood)  
**Dark Pets:** Varook (Gloomwood), Charnelix (Sable Mire), Kaldraun (Frozen Tundra), Khalsarra (Cracked Earth)

---

# 1) Calanthor-native Lineages (official)
These are setting-signature lineages with written mechanics.

## Aeris (Skyborn)
**Silhouette tell:** lean wind-adapted ridgefolk; chimes, cords, gliding posture.  
**Typical Spirit (by birthplace):** Light (Keairvari), sometimes Dark (Khalsarra ridge-borders).  
**Note:** Born Under the Pet overrides.

## Emberkin (Cinder-Blooded)
**Silhouette tell:** soot-braid traditions; heat-safe wraps; ember charms; ash-sight.  
**Typical Spirit (by birthplace):** Light (Nyvakira), sometimes Dark (Charnelix border refugees).  
**Note:** Born Under the Pet overrides.

# 2) Standard 5e Species → Calanthor Reskin Matrix
These are reskins. Use standard 5e mechanics and swap only names/lore.

## Dwarves (still unmistakably dwarf)
**Instant dwarf tells:** short + dense; stone/metal craft; stubborn vitality; oath/forge culture.

### Hill Dwarf → Cairn-Root Dwarves
- **Calanthor name:** Cairn-Root Dwarves  
- **Typical homes:** Kaldraun border shelters, cairn networks  
- **Typical Spirit:** Dark (Kaldraun)  
- **Reskin tells:** cairn law, shelter rites, map-stones

### Mountain Dwarf → Monolith-Forged Dwarves
- **Calanthor name:** Monolith-Forged Dwarves  
- **Typical homes:** Khalsarra monolith belts, cracked-earth strongholds  
- **Typical Spirit:** Dark (Khalsarra)  
- **Reskin tells:** oathstone scars, heavy craft, burden rites

### Any Dwarf (variant) → Ember-Anvil Dwarves
- **Typical homes:** Emberwood forgeholds, caravan anvils  
- **Typical Spirit:** Light (Nyvakira)  
- **Reskin tells:** heat-tempered beard rings, ember hospitality

---

## Elves (still unmistakably elf)
**Instant elf tells:** tall/lithe; keen senses; ancient poise; ritual precision.

### High Elf → Veil-Scribed Elves
- **Typical homes:** treaty cities, peak archives, wind-script halls  
- **Typical Spirit:** Light (Keairvari/Shakora)  
- **Reskin tells:** written omen traditions, precise speech, scholarly rites

### Wood Elf → Grove-Warden Elves
- **Typical homes:** Verdant Valley steward sites  
- **Typical Spirit:** Light (Gaianok)  
- **Reskin tells:** mutualist ethics, leafcraft, quiet guardianship

### Drow → Hushborne Elves
- **Typical homes:** Varook border towns and hush sanctuaries (not deep Gloomwood)  
- **Typical Spirit:** Dark (Varook)  
- **Reskin tells:** name-taboo customs, hush tattoos, memory wards

---

## Halflings (still unmistakably halfling)
**Instant halfling tells:** small, brave, lucky, community survival.

### Lightfoot → Wayfarer Halflings
- **Typical homes:** trade roads, treaty corridors  
- **Typical Spirit:** Light (Shakora)  
- **Reskin tells:** hidden routes, “always elsewhere,” messenger webs

### Stout → Hearth-Stout Halflings
- **Typical homes:** harsh-zone edges (tundra/cracked earth caravans)  
- **Typical Spirit:** Mixed (often Dark by border birth)
