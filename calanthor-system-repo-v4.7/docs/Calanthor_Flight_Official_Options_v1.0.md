# Calanthor Flight System — Official Options (v1.0)
*Cinematic, scalable flight that stays fair at low levels.*

This document turns the kernel’s **Aerial Mastery ladder** + **Aerial Strain** into **official character options**:
- a playable **Aeris ancestry feature set**
- **feats** that let non-Aeris characters earn flight gradually
- clear **armor/encumbrance** rules
- clear **checks/DCs** for aerial risk

Use these options with the **Calanthor D&D Baseline v1.0**.

---

## 1) Core mechanic (always the same)
### Aerial Strain (0–6)
Each flying creature tracks **Aerial Strain** individually.

**Gain Aerial Strain**
- Flying while **medium armor** or **encumbered**: +1 (each scene you fly)
- Flying while **heavy armor**: prohibited unless feature says otherwise
- Taking damage while airborne: +1
- Flying in **severe conditions** (storm/whiteout/spores/veilstorm): +1 per scene
- Exceeding your tier’s allowed duration: +1 per round beyond the limit

**Thresholds**
- **2:** disadvantage on your next Acrobatics check made to maneuver or land safely
- **4:** flying speed halved until a short rest (or you must land at end of your turn — GM choice)
- **6:** **Forced Landing** at end of your turn; gain **Minor Strain**; cannot fly again until a short rest

**Recovery**
- Short rest in safe shelter: -2 Aerial Strain
- Long rest: Aerial Strain resets to 0

## 2) Aerial Mastery tiers (rules text)
These tiers describe what flight *can do* at each character level band.

### Tier 0 (Levels 1–4): Jump + Glide
**Jump-Enhanced.** You have advantage on Athletics checks made to jump or climb in open terrain (GM discretion).  
**Glide (Reaction).** When you fall, you can glide: reduce falling damage by an amount equal to **5 × your character level**, and you may steer up to **10 feet horizontally per 10 feet** fallen.  
You cannot gain altitude and cannot hover.

### Tier 1 (Levels 5–8): Burst Flight
**Burst Flight (1/Short Rest).** As part of your movement, you can fly up to your Speed in a straight line (you may turn up to 45° once). You must end on solid ground.  
If you take damage during Burst Flight, make a **DC 10 CON save** or descend safely (forced landing at end of movement).

### Tier 2 (Levels 9–12): Short Sustained Flight
**Sustained Flight (PB/Long Rest).** As a bonus action, you gain a flying speed equal to your walking speed for **1 minute**.  
You cannot hover for free: if you end your turn airborne, you must have moved at least **10 ft** that turn (otherwise you descend 10 ft).  
**Medium armor**: each scene you use Sustained Flight, gain +1 additional Aerial Strain.

### Tier 3 (Levels 13–16): Tactical Flight
Your Sustained Flight improves:
- You may hover if you spend **10 ft** movement that turn.
- You may carry **light gear** without extra strain (still no medium/heavy carry).

### Tier 4 (Levels 17+): Mastery
Your flight becomes fully tactical:
- Hovering is reliable (no descent requirement if you take at least 5 ft movement).
- You may carry **one willing creature** your size or smaller at **half flying speed** (GM may call for STR(Athletics) checks in storms).

## 3) Official option: Aeris (Winged Ancestry)
Use this as a **species trait package** (5e ancestry-style).

### Aeris Traits (core)
- **Ability Score:** use your table’s standard species method (or +2 DEX, +1 WIS suggested).
- **Speed:** 30 ft.
- **Wind Sense:** you have proficiency in **Perception** or **Survival** (choose one).
- **Ritecraft:** you gain proficiency with **Ritecraft tools**.
- **Skyborn Physiology:** you gain **Tier 0 (Jump + Glide)** at level 1.
- **Aerial Mastery Unlock:** you gain the higher tiers automatically at levels **5 / 9 / 13 / 17**.
- **Armor Limit:** you can’t use features from Tier 1+ while wearing **heavy armor**.
  In **medium armor**, you gain +1 Aerial Strain each scene you fly.

### Why this is balanced
At low levels, Aeris are safer around falls and better at vertical terrain, but they do not bypass walls/encounters. Real flight arrives at the same levels many characters gain strong mobility or battlefield control.

## 4) Official option: Feats (earning flight)
These feats let any character develop aerial capability without breaking low-level play.

### FEAT: Glide Adept
**Prerequisite:** DEX 13 or Athletics proficiency  
You gain **Tier 0 (Jump + Glide)**. If you already have Tier 0, you instead gain:
- advantage on Acrobatics checks to land safely
- and you reduce Aerial Strain recovery penalty by +1 on short rest (short rest reduces by 3 instead of 2).

### FEAT: Burst Flyer
**Prerequisite:** Level 5+, Glide Adept or Tier 0 from another source  
You gain **Tier 1 (Burst Flight)** (1/short rest).

### FEAT: Sustained Flyer
**Prerequisite:** Level 9+, Burst Flyer  
You gain **Tier 2 (Short Sustained Flight)** (PB/long rest).

### FEAT: Sky Tactician
**Prerequisite:** Level 13+, Sustained Flyer  
You gain **Tier 3 (Tactical Flight)** and:
- once per long rest, when you would gain Aerial Strain, you can ignore that instance.

### FEAT: Sky Master
**Prerequisite:** Level 17+, Sky Tactician  
You gain **Tier 4 (Mastery)**.

## 5) Official option: Class hooks (optional)
If you want flight to feel “earned by training,” you can offer these as class features instead of feats.

### Fighter (Martial Archetype feature, Level 9+)
**Aerial Drill.** You gain Tier 1 (Burst Flight). At level 13, gain Tier 2. At level 17, gain Tier 3.

### Monk (Level 9+)
**Wind Step.** While unarmored, you can use Tier 1 Burst Flight PB/long rest. At level 13, Tier 2.

### Ranger (Level 9+, in Skyward Peaks campaigns)
**Thermal Rider.** When in open air, you can activate Tier 2 Sustained Flight once per long rest without gaining Aerial Strain from severe wind (still from damage).

(These hooks are optional and campaign-specific. If you already use core 5e flight spells/features, keep them as written.)

## 6) Checks and DCs for aerial danger (D&D-native)
Use these when flight is risky (storms, tight canyons, veilstorms, spore clouds).

### Maneuver Check (DEX Acrobatics)
- **DC 10** calm air, wide open
- **DC 15** gusts, moderate obstacles
- **DC 20** severe storm/whiteout/spores, tight terrain
- **DC 25** veilstorm / Great Pet attention moment

On failure: forced descent 10–30 ft (GM), and +1 Aerial Strain.

### Carry / Rescue Check (STR Athletics)
- **DC 15** carry a willing creature briefly (Tier 4 only; otherwise impossible)
- **DC 20** carry in storm or while injured

### Damage While Airborne (CON Save)
If you take damage while flying and you are **sustaining** (Tier 2+), make a CON save:
- **DC 10 or half the damage (whichever is higher)**
On failure: forced descent/landing (GM) and +1 Aerial Strain.

(Burst Flight uses DC 10 flat as written in Tier 1.)

## 7) Region and Domain Pressure interaction (quick)
- **Keairvari (Skyward Peaks) active storms:** severe conditions more often → Aerial Strain triggers more.
- **Kaldraun (Frozen Tundra) veilstorms:** treat as DC 20–25 aerial maneuvers; failure can cause separation.
- **Varook (Gloomwood):** flight doesn’t negate memory pressure; visibility isn’t safety.
- **Charnelix (Sable Mire):** spore clouds count as severe conditions; flight may avoid ground hazards but increases strain risk.

---

## Recommended default (best-feeling setup)
- Aeris get **Tier 0 at level 1** and unlock tiers at **5/9/13/17**.
- Non-Aeris must take feats to gain flight tiers.
- Use Aerial Strain and the Maneuver DC table whenever flight would trivialize a scene.
