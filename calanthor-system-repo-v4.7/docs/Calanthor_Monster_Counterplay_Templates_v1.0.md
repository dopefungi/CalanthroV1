# Calanthor Monster Gen v1 — Counterplay Template Library (v1.0)
*Counterplay is mandatory. Pressure must be playable, not punitive.*

This library provides **plug-and-play counterplay** that matches:
- elemental grafts (FIRE/AIR/WATER/ICE/EARTH/VERDANT)
- domain threshold packages (KALDRAUN_STASIS / KHALSARRA_WEIGHT / CHARNELIX_BLIGHT / VAROOK_SILENCE)
- Calanthor’s Procedure Check philosophy (bad rolls still move play forward)

---

## 0) Counterplay rules (official)
### 0.1 Minimum counterplay per monster
- **Standard:** 2 counterplays
- **Elite:** 3 counterplays
- **Boss:** 4 counterplays (at least 1 must be a *choice/bargain* counter)

### 0.2 Counterplay diversity rule
A monster’s counterplay must include **at least two different categories**:
1) **Resource** (gear, prep, consumables, terrain use)
2) **Skill/Action** (Ritecraft/Medicine/Survival/Athletics/Arcana/etc.)
3) **Choice/Bargain** (retreat, vow, parley, sacrifice, reroute)

### 0.3 The “No Shut-Down” rule
Counterplay should **reduce pressure** or **create openings**, not delete the monster.
Good counterplay outcomes:
- end a zone for 1 round
- grant advantage on the next save/attack
- reduce overlay ticks or prevent one threshold effect
- force the monster to reposition or lose a trait briefly

### 0.4 Tell → Counterplay link (clarity)
If a monster has a “tell” (spores swell, stones hum, pressure drop), the counterplay should reference it:
> “When you see X, you can do Y.”

### 0.5 Scaling by track (2/4/6)
If an overlay is active, counterplay should scale:
- **Track 2:** simple, accessible counterplay (torch, cloth mask, cairn line)
- **Track 4:** counterplay requires better prep or costs (water, rite chalk, fatigue)
- **Track 6:** counterplay still works, but usually only once, or with a cost/event

# 1) Universal Counterplay Templates (works for any monster)
## Resource
**U-R1 — Break Line-of-Effect**  
Use cover, smoke, mist, or terrain to break targeting; ends one “no reactions” or “disadvantage on next roll” rider (GM).

**U-R2 — Safety Kit Use**  
Spend a kit charge (antitoxin, cold-weather gear, respirator mask, rope pitons) to gain advantage on the next relevant save this scene.

## Skill/Action
**U-S1 — Read the Pattern (Action)**  
Make a relevant check (Perception/Insight/Survival/Arcana/Ritecraft) DC = monster DC +2.  
On success: the next ally to target the monster gains advantage OR ignores a zone penalty once.

**U-S2 — Counter-stance (Action)**  
Athletics/Acrobatics DC = monster DC.  
On success: end difficult terrain in a 10-ft square OR prevent prone/restrain from the next effect this round.

## Choice/Bargain
**U-C1 — Detour / Retreat**  
Retreat 30 ft or change route; reduce overlay track by 1 **or** avoid a track 6 event at the cost of time/resources.

**U-C2 — Trade Something Real**  
Offer a resource, vow, or concession (GM adjudicates).  
On acceptance: monster pauses/redirects one turn OR loses one trait for 1 round.

# 2) Element Counterplay Sets
Pick 1 counterplay that matches the monster’s primary element (or its signature graft).

## FIRE counterplay
**F-R1 — Smother & Starve** *(Resource)*  
Throw sand/blanket or move into wet ground; ends a FIRE zone (cinder line / flare) for 1 round.

**F-S1 — Vent the Heat (Skill/Action)**  
Ritecraft or Survival (action), DC = monster DC.  
On success: prevent “no reactions” rider from the next FIRE hit this round.

**F-C1 — Answer With Courage (Choice)**  
Declare a bold purpose and act on it (attack/rescue/advance).  
On success (GM): convert a fear/flare consequence into **Minor Strain** instead of losing actions.

## AIR counterplay
**A-R1 — Anchor Up** *(Resource)*  
Use rope/pitons/shields/bracing; gain advantage on the next forced-movement or prone save this scene.

**A-S1 — Read the Gust (Skill/Action)**  
Survival or Perception (action), DC = monster DC.  
On success: you and one ally ignore wind difficult terrain and forced-movement push distance is halved for 1 round.

**A-C1 — Give Ground** *(Choice)*  
You choose to be pushed/prone (accept the reposition) to avoid the rider: you don’t lose reactions/silence effects this time.

## WATER counterplay
**W-R1 — Dry Footing** *(Resource)*  
Spread ash/sand or take higher ground; end slick terrain/difficult terrain patch in one 10-ft square.

**W-S1 — Break the Bind (Skill/Action)**  
Athletics (action) DC = monster DC.  
On success: one restrained/slow effect becomes only -10 speed until end of turn.

**W-C1 — Share Breath** *(Choice)*  
An ally spends a reaction to steady you (grabs you / grounds you): you reroll one STR save vs pull/bind (must take new result).

## ICE counterplay
**I-R1 — Heat & Shelter** *(Resource)*  
Use a torch, hot pack, or proper shelter moment: remove **Chilled** from one creature OR reduce a whiteout aura effect for 1 round.

**I-S1 — Breath Discipline (Skill/Action)**  
Constitution check or Survival (action), DC = monster DC.  
On success: you ignore -10 speed and disadvantage from cold effects for 1 round.

**I-C1 — Slow Is Smooth** *(Choice)*  
You halve your speed willingly until end of your next turn; in exchange you gain advantage on the next save vs restrain/prone from ICE.

## EARTH counterplay
**E-R1 — Find the Fault** *(Resource)*  
Use a prybar/hammer or exploit cracks: you can destroy one 5-ft “cover” or pillar feature created by the monster (if present).

**E-S1 — Break the Anchor (Skill/Action)**  
Athletics (action) or Ritecraft (action), DC = monster DC.  
On success: end Anchor Field / difficult terrain in a 10-ft radius for 1 round.

**E-C1 — Vow of Motion (Choice)**  
Declare a purpose (“I will reach X no matter the weight”) and move toward it.  
Until end of turn you ignore Sacred Weight-style speed reductions, but you take Minor Strain.

## VERDANT counterplay
**V-R1 — Mask & Wash** *(Resource)*  
Spend a cloth mask charge or cleansing water: gain advantage on next CON save vs spores/blight this scene.

**V-S1 — Sac Targeting (Skill/Action)**  
Medicine or Nature (action), DC = monster DC.  
On success: the next spore/blight action this scene gives targets advantage on the save.

**V-C1 — Controlled Burn (Choice)**  
You scorch the ground (fire, salt, etc.) at a cost: you end a spore zone for 1 round but lose 5 ft speed next turn (or consume a resource).

# 3) Domain Counterplay Sets (Track-aware)
Pick 1 counterplay that matches the monster’s domain threshold package when applicable.

## KALDRAUN_STASIS counterplay
**K-R1 — Bright Line (Resource)**  
Hold bright flame (torch/bonfire) within 10 ft: ends “Hide in whiteout” or reduces whiteout aura for 1 round.

**K-S1 — Cairn Reading (Skill/Action)**  
Survival (action) DC = monster DC +2.  
On success: identify a safe drift-line; your party prevents **one** Track 2 effect this obstacle.

**K-C1 — Tight Formation (Choice)**  
You choose to stay within 10 ft of an ally until end of next round; you gain advantage on saves vs push/prone from Track 6 gusts, but you’re easier to catch in cones.

## KHALSARRA_WEIGHT counterplay
**KH-R1 — Witness Chalk (Resource)**  
Spend chalk/ash to mark a boundary: creatures inside gain advantage on saves vs **Weighed** for 1 round.

**KH-S1 — Witness Line (Skill/Action)**  
Ritecraft or Persuasion (action) DC = monster DC.  
On success: you “name the burden” — prevent the next Track 4 aura upgrade this obstacle.

**KH-C1 — Declare Purpose (Choice)**  
Declare a vow aligned with the scene and follow it immediately with an action.  
On success (GM): reduce Weight pressure on you by 1 step (Weighed ends or becomes speed -10). Cost: Minor Strain if you break the vow later.

## CHARNELIX_BLIGHT counterplay
**C-R1 — Clean Water / Fire (Resource)**  
Spend 1 gallon clean water OR apply fire: remove **Blighted** from one creature (Track 4+: requires DC 15 CON save as written in prior exemplars).

**C-S1 — Antidote Craft (Skill/Action)**  
Medicine (action) DC = monster DC +2.  
On success: you prepare a quick binder; one ally gains advantage on CON saves vs Blight until end of scene.

**C-C1 — Cut the Web (Choice)**  
You destroy terrain (burn/salt) to make a lane.  
End one difficult terrain patch but you attract attention: the domain track ticks +1 OR you take Minor Strain (GM choice).

## VAROOK_SILENCE counterplay
**V-R1 — Name-Cord (Resource)**  
Use a name-charm cord or written name token: ignore one application of Haunted/Silenced this scene for one creature.

**V-S1 — Recall Anchor (Skill/Action)**  
Insight or History (action) DC = monster DC.  
On success: you anchor reality; reduce the duration of Haunted/Silenced applied by this monster by 1 step (GM: 10 min→1 min, 1 min→1 round, 1 round→ends after this turn).

**V-C1 — Speak the Truth (Choice)**  
You confess a relevant truth (or accept a fear) aloud.  
On success (GM): you prevent the next Track 6 hush burst this obstacle, but you gain a visible mark/omen (story cost).

# 4) Counterplay selection heuristics (generator rules)
- Always pick at least one counterplay that targets the monster’s **signature action**.
- If the monster applies **Medium control**, include a counterplay that can **end it early**.
- If the monster has a **zone**, include a resource counter that can **clear a square**.
- If the monster is **Elite/Boss**, ensure at least one counterplay is a **choice/bargain** option.

---

# 5) Output format for generated monsters (minimum)
Each monster should list counterplay like this:

**Counterplay**
- **Resource:** <template name> — <exact effect>  
- **Skill/Action:** <template name> — <exact effect>  
- **Choice:** <template name> — <exact effect>

Keep it short and actionable at the table.
