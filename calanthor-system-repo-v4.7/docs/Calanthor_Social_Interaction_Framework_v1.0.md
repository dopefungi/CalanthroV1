# Calanthor Social Interaction Framework (v1.0)
*A D&D-style social system that stays fast at the table, but has Calanthor “bite”: Witness rites, vows, and Pet-alignment pressure.*

This module is built to work with:
- **Procedure Check Usage Rules** (fail-forward, momentum)
- **Spirit Alignment Rules** (Born Under the Pet)
- **Equipment tiers** (witness chalk, law seals, namecords)
- **Domain overlays** (social pressure can be “weather” in courts and borders)

---

## 1) Core principles (official)
1) **Social scenes are obstacles**, not pass/fail gates. Low rolls move play forward with cost.
2) **Truth + Leverage beats math.** Good leverage can reduce DCs or skip rolls.
3) **Vows matter in Calanthor.** Vows are power, reputation, and sometimes trap.
4) **Witness changes reality.** If something is witnessed/recorded, it becomes harder to deny—social “terrain.”

---

## 2) Social scene structure (the 3-step loop)
### Step A — Set the objective
Examples:
- “Secure passage through the Keairvari gate.”
- “Get the council to delay judgment.”
- “Extract a confession without breaking treaty law.”

### Step B — Choose the frame (what kind of scene is this?)
- **Parley** (deal-making, alliances)
- **Investigation** (truth-finding, rumors)
- **Confrontation** (intimidation, public pressure)
- **Rite** (witness lines, oaths, petitions)
- **Deception** (misdirection, cover stories)

### Step C — Run it as an Obstacle with a track
Use **Disposition Track 0–6** (like overlays), with 2/4/6 beats.

**Disposition Track**
- **0–1** hostile / closed
- **2–3** guarded / listening
- **4–5** favorable / negotiating
- **6** concession / agreement (or “resolution”)

> The GM can start the track anywhere based on fiction (hostile court starts 1; friendly steward starts 3–4).

**Track events**
- **At 2:** someone offers a *test* or a small price.
- **At 4:** a real opportunity opens (or the other side reveals their true demand).
- **At 6:** resolution: agreement, treaty seal, passage, confession, etc.

---

## 3) Rolls, DCs, and what to ask for
### 3.1 Default DCs
Use 5e-adjacent DCs, then adjust with leverage:
- Easy 10, Standard 13, Hard 16, Extreme 19

### 3.2 What check to call for
- **Persuasion**: bargains, appeals, calming, coalition building
- **Deception**: cover story, forged narrative
- **Intimidation**: threat, dominance, fear pressure
- **Insight**: read motive, detect leverage, predict pushback
- **Investigation**: connect facts, prove contradictions
- **History**: precedent, treaty clauses, old vows
- **Ritecraft**: witness lines, lawful petitions, binding oaths
- **Performance**: public sway, ritual delivery, crowd scenes

### 3.3 Leverage (Calanthor’s social advantage)
Leverage is a concrete thing you can name.
Examples:
- **Proof** (document, relic, witness)
- **Need** (they’re hungry for something you have)
- **Authority** (seal, office, rite permission)
- **Reputation** (faction standing, deed)
- **Fear** (credible threat / consequences)
- **Mercy** (you can spare them)

**Leverage effects (pick 1):**
- reduce DC by 2–5
- replace a roll with automatic +1 track (if leverage is overwhelming)
- allow a different skill (History instead of Persuasion, etc.)
- prevent one “cost” on a failure (once)

---

## 4) Fail-forward results (Procedure compatible)
When a check fails, choose 1 (or roll d6):
1) **Concession:** you gain +1 track but pay a price (coin/favor/time)
2) **Complication:** you gain information but attract attention
3) **Delay:** progress stalls; clock advances or opportunity shrinks
4) **Mark:** you gain +1 track but gain a social tag (Suspected, Owed)
5) **Escalation:** opposition gains a move (guards called, terms worsen)
6) **Split:** you succeed with one faction but offend another

---

## 5) Social Tags (conditions for people and groups)
Social tags are short, readable labels the GM can attach to NPCs/factions.  
They interact with rolls and scenes, not combat.

**Common tags**
- **Witnessed:** claim/action is recorded; harder to deny; leverage for the holder
- **Owed:** you owe a favor (or they owe you)
- **Suspected:** guards/clerks watch you; Deception DC +2 against that group
- **Trusted:** Persuasion DC -2 with that group
- **Marked (Light/Dark):** visible omen or Pet association; affects court reactions

**Clearing tags**
- pay the debt, produce proof, perform a rite of clarification, or spend time repairing reputation

---

## 6) Vows and Witness Rites (the Calanthor slice)
### 6.1 Declaring a vow
A PC can declare a vow in a social scene:
- “I swear passage will not be used to harm your people.”
- “I swear to return the relic to the cairn.”

**Mechanical use (light touch):**
- If the vow is relevant and witnessed, you may gain **+1 track** (once/scene) *or* reduce DC by 2.
- If the vow is broken later, the GM may apply **Owed**, **Marked**, or advance a relevant domain overlay.

### 6.2 Witness lines
Using **Rite Chalk & Cord** or lawful tokens, a PC can establish a witness line:
- creates a “social terrain” where lying is riskier
- makes agreements harder to later dispute

**Ritecraft check** (DC as scene): on success, one of:
- Deception in the line is DC +2
- Agreements made in the line count as “sealed” (Witnessed tag)

---

## 7) Social “Moves” for NPCs (GM quick list)
Use these instead of over-rolling:
- **Offer a test** (prove competence)
- **Demand collateral** (hostage, bond, vow)
- **Invoke precedent** (History pressure)
- **Public shame** (Performance pressure)
- **Divide the party** (different offers)
- **Call a witness** (shift the terrain)
- **Delay with bureaucracy** (clock pressure)

---

## 8) Scene templates (copy/paste)
### 8.1 Gate Parley (border crossing)
- Objective: passage
- Track start: 1–3
- Track 2: inspection/test
- Track 4: terms revealed (fees, vow, escort)
- Track 6: sealed pass OR denied with consequence

### 8.2 Council Hearing (treaty city)
- Objective: judgment delay/mercy/permission
- Track start: 0–2
- Track 2: faction speaks (pick side)
- Track 4: precedent invoked (History/Ritecraft)
- Track 6: ruling (plus tags: Witnessed, Owed)

### 8.3 Interrogation (truth extraction)
- Objective: confession or contradiction
- Track start: 1–2
- Use: Insight/Investigation, not just Intimidation
- Track 4: soft break (partial truth)
- Track 6: full confession OR lie exposed (Witnessed)

---

## 9) Minimal outputs (what to write at table)
- Objective
- Track start
- 1–3 leverage bullets
- 2–3 NPC moves likely
- Tags gained/cleared

That’s it—keep it moving.
