# Calanthor Species & Culture Framework (v1.0)
*A 5e-compatible character identity model that keeps physiology, culture, and region power separate (and balanced).*

This framework solves a common setting problem:
- If **culture** gives combat power, it power-creeps.
- If **species** carries all the lore, characters feel same-y.
- If **region** grants big bonuses, the map becomes a buff menu.

So Calanthor uses **three layers**:

1) **Species (Lineage):** physiology and ancestry traits (low-to-moderate power)
2) **Culture (Background):** upbringing, rites, laws, and social access (low power, high utility)
3) **Region Attunement (optional):** small resonance package (already in your packs)

---

## 1) Design principles (official)
- **Species** gives what your body can do.
- **Culture** gives what your people taught you (skills, tool, contacts, rites).
- **Region Attunement** gives small, scene-based resonance (1/scene + 1/short rest surge).
- No layer should invalidate obstacles by itself at low levels.
- When in doubt: give **options and access** more than flat power.

---

## 2) Character creation (Calanthor steps)
1. Build character using 5e rules (class, abilities, equipment).
2. Choose **Species/Lineage** (5e list + optional Calanthor lineages below).
3. Choose **Culture Background** (Calanthor cultures below, or any 5e background reskinned).
4. Optionally choose a **Region Attunement** (from the packs) if the campaign uses them.
5. Record your **tags** (primary element preference, domain motifs, ritecraft).

---

## 3) Species/Lineage policy
### A) Standard 5e species are allowed (reskin encouraged)
You may use any standard 5e species (human, dwarf, elf, halfling, gnome, orc, etc.).
Calanthor re-flavors them by *where they live and who they serve*.

Examples:
- Mountain dwarves might be **Khalsarra Monolith-Born** clans (same rules, new lore).
- Wood elves might be **Gaianok Grove-Wardens** (same rules, new lore).
- Tieflings might be **Veil-Touched** from old rift scars (same rules, new lore).

### B) Calanthor-native lineages (optional)
Use these when you want the setting to feel distinct without rewriting 5e.

#### Lineage: Aeris (Skyborn)
**Core identity:** wind-adapted people of the peaks.  
**Power goal:** mobility + scouting, with flight progression balanced by tiers/strain.

**Traits (recommended):**
- Speed 30 ft
- Choose one: proficiency in **Perception** or **Survival**
- **Ritecraft tool proficiency**
- **Skyborn Physiology:** Tier 0 (Jump + Glide) at level 1
- **Aerial Mastery unlocks:** tiers at **5 / 9 / 13 / 17** (see Flight Official Options)
- Armor limits: heavy armor blocks Tier 1+; medium armor adds +1 Aerial Strain per scene flying

*(Full flight rules live in `docs/Calanthor_Flight_Official_Options_v1.0.md`.)*

#### Lineage: Emberkin (Cinder-Blooded)
**Core identity:** Emberwood-adapted people shaped by heat ecology and renewal rites.  
**Power goal:** survivability in heat + minor fire resonance, not raw damage scaling.

**Traits (recommended):**
- Speed 30 ft
- **Heat-Hardened:** resistance to **fire damage** OR ignore extreme heat penalties (choose one)
- **Ash-Sight:** you can see normally through smoke/ash out to 30 ft (heavily obscured still blocks beyond)
- **Ritecraft tool proficiency**
- **Renewal Spark (1/Long Rest):** when you drop to 0 HP, you may drop to 1 HP instead and gain Minor Strain (your body “restarts” once)

**Notes:** If you allow fire resistance, avoid stacking too many other resistances at low levels.

### C) “Culture is not Species” (official stance)
The following are **cultures/backgrounds**, not lineages, unless you intentionally want physiology changes:
- Frostborn
- Ashwalkers
- Shakoran River-Law peoples
- Gaianok stewards

This keeps character options flexible and avoids “only humans can be Frostborn.”

## 4) Culture Backgrounds (Calanthor)
Each background follows 5e expectations:
- 2 skills
- 1 tool proficiency (often Ritecraft)
- 1 language (or vehicle/tool swap)
- a Background Feature (access/contacts/rights)
- a small **Cultural Gift** (1/scene utility) — optional but recommended for flavor

### Background: Frostborn Whisperer (Kaldraun)
- Skills: Survival, Perception
- Tool: Ritecraft
- Language: one of your choice
- Feature: **Cairn Rights** — Frostborn shelters recognize your marks; you can request safe shelter once per travel leg (GM may attach a duty).
- Cultural Gift (1/scene): **Read the Cracks** — add +PB to a check to interpret ice, omen-lights, or whiteout routes.

### Background: Ashwalker Vow-Bearer (Khalsarra)
- Skills: Insight, Survival
- Tool: Ritecraft
- Language: one of your choice
- Feature: **Oath-Witness** — in Khalsarra lands, communities will hear a vow and grant a single practical aid if the vow is just.
- Cultural Gift (1/scene): **Declare Purpose** — add +PB to a check made to resist despair, endure hardship, or negotiate mercy.

### Background: Shakoran Mediator (Waters)
- Skills: Persuasion, Insight
- Tool: Ritecraft (Purification kit) or Calligrapher’s supplies (choose one)
- Language: one of your choice
- Feature: **River-Law** — you know water-right customs; you can usually secure a hearing before violence (unless taboo is broken).
- Cultural Gift (1/scene): **Clean Hands** — add +PB to a check to cleanse, de-escalate, or establish a neutral boundary.

### Background: Gaianok Steward (Verdant Valley)
- Skills: Nature, Medicine
- Tool: Herbalism kit or Ritecraft (choose one)
- Language: one of your choice
- Feature: **Steward’s Claim** — you can ask for seeds/remedies and basic lodging from steward circles if you pledge restitution.
- Cultural Gift (1/scene): **Mutualism** — add +PB to a check to calm beasts, identify safe plants, or prevent ecological harm.

### Background: Emberwood Rite-Dancer (Nyvakira)
- Skills: Athletics, Intimidation or Performance (choose one)
- Tool: Ritecraft
- Language: one of your choice
- Feature: **Emberkin Hospitality** — Emberwood enclaves grant shelter if you respect renewal rites; breaking taboo voids this.
- Cultural Gift (1/scene): **Vent the Heat** — add +PB to a check to cross heat hazards, stabilize a flare-up, or craft safe fire.

### Background: Aeris Sky-Sentinel (Keairvari)
- Skills: Acrobatics, Perception
- Tool: Navigator’s tools or Ritecraft (choose one)
- Language: one of your choice
- Feature: **Wind-Writ Access** — Aeris outposts will share route omens if you offer truthful news or service.
- Cultural Gift (1/scene): **Wind-Read** — add +PB to a check to predict storms or find a safe ridge path.

### Threshold Culture (optional): Mire Marshrunner (Charnelix border)
- Skills: Stealth, Medicine
- Tool: Poisoner’s kit or Herbalism kit (choose one)
- Feature: **Stilt-Camp Routes** — you know two safe “web-lane” crossings (GM places them).
- Cultural Gift (1/scene): **Field Antitoxin** — add +PB to a check to mitigate spores/poison (does not replace full cures).

### Threshold Culture (optional): Gloomward Witness (Varook border)
- Skills: Religion, Insight
- Tool: Ritecraft
- Feature: **Witness Rite** — you can establish a basic name-ward circle during a short rest (prevents one Haunted application once per rest).
- Cultural Gift (1/scene): **Anchor the Name** — add +PB to a check to resist fear/memory manipulation.

---

## 5) Region Attunement integration (don’t double-dip)
If the campaign uses Region Attunements:
- you may take **one** attunement (your “homeland”)
- your Culture background may match or differ from your attunement (refugees, adoptees, travelers)

**Balance rule:** If a background’s Cultural Gift and a Region Attunement passive would apply to the same roll, you choose **one** (no stacking) unless the GM explicitly allows.

---

## 6) Conversion guidance for existing 5e backgrounds
If you want to reskin a 5e background as Calanthor culture:
- keep the mechanical proficiencies and feature
- add a Cultural Gift (1/scene +PB) that fits the culture

This keeps everything compatible and fast.

---

## 7) Recommended default (best-feeling)
- Allow all standard 5e species.
- Offer **Aeris** and **Emberkin** as optional Calanthor-native lineages.
- Treat **Frostborn/Ashwalker/Shakoran/Gaianok** as backgrounds (cultures).
- Use Region Attunements only if the campaign benefits from resonance bonuses.
