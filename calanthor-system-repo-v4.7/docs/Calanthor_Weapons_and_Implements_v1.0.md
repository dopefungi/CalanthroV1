# Calanthor Weapons & Implements (v1.0)
*A D&D-compatible weapon list with Calanthor tags + non-damage “resonance hooks” that complement techniques, spells, and Procedure Checks.*

This module is **5e-readable**: damage dice and properties work like standard weapons.
Calanthor adds:
- **Tags** (region/culture/spirit) for quick lore alignment
- **Resonance Hooks** (small utility, not attack/damage bonuses) that integrate with techniques/spells and keep balance intact

---

## 1) Rules: what makes a weapon “Calanthor” (official)
### 1.1 Baseline stats are normal
A weapon’s **damage** and **properties** should match a familiar 5e baseline profile.

### 1.2 Calanthor tags (for generators + GM flavor)
Each weapon can have tags like:
- `REGION:KALDRAUN`, `REGION:KHALSARRA`, `REGION:CHARNELIX`, `REGION:VAROOK`
- `CULTURE:Frostborn`, `CULTURE:Ashwalker`, `CULTURE:Gaianok`, etc.
- `SPIRIT:LIGHT` or `SPIRIT:DARK` (optional; usually for relic-grade items)

### 1.3 Resonance Hooks (no combat-math creep)
Resonance Hooks **must not** add flat bonuses to hit, damage, or AC.
Instead, they:
- grant advantage on a *specific* Procedure Check
- reduce/shift **Minor Strain** (rare, 1/long rest)
- improve **counterplay** (clear a zone, end a rider 1 round)
- create **tells** (signal, light, omen)

### 1.4 One-Modifier Rule applies
If a hook would grant advantage or +PB on a roll, it cannot stack with Culture Gifts / Class Edges / Region Resonance on the same roll.

# 3) Calanthor Signature Weapon Variants (reskins + hooks)
These are **recognizable** (you instantly see the base weapon), but Calanthor-flavored and generator-friendly.

## Khalsarra (Cracked Earth / Sacred Weight)
- **Oathstone Maul** *(Maul)* — Tags: REGION:KHALSARRA, SPIRIT:DARK (common)  
  **Hook (1/Long Rest):** When you succeed on an Athletics check to resist forced movement or to shove, you may prevent **one** overlay tick from KHALSARRA_WEIGHT this obstacle (your stance steadies the line).

- **Faultline Pike** *(Pike)* — Tags: REGION:KHALSARRA  
  **Hook (1/Scene):** When you hit a creature inside difficult terrain you created, you may push it 5 ft (no save). (No extra damage.)

## Kaldraun (Frozen Tundra / Stasis)
- **Driftglass Dagger** *(Dagger)* — Tags: REGION:KALDRAUN, SPIRIT:DARK (rare)  
  **Hook (1/Scene):** If you begin your turn in snow/fog, you can attempt to Hide (no action) before moving (must still meet normal hiding rules).

- **Cairn-Hammer** *(Warhammer)* — Tags: REGION:KALDRAUN, CULTURE:Frostborn  
  **Hook:** Advantage on **Survival** checks to build emergency shelter or read drift-lines (Procedure Checks; One-Modifier Rule applies).

## Charnelix border (Sable Mire / Blight)
- **Sporehook Sickle** *(Sickle)* — Tags: REGION:CHARNELIX, SPIRIT:DARK  
  **Hook (1/Short Rest):** As an action, scrape and burn growth from a 5-ft square to end one spore/difficult-terrain patch for 1 round.

- **Mireline Net** *(Net)* — Tags: REGION:CHARNELIX, REGION:SHAKORA (common in patrols)  
  **Hook:** When used as counterplay, a successful Athletics check to restrain a creature also grants advantage on the next **Medicine** check to treat contamination this scene.

## Varook border (Hush / Memory pressure)
- **Namecord Whip** *(Whip)* — Tags: REGION:VAROOK, SPIRIT:DARK (uncommon)  
  **Hook (1/Long Rest):** When you would become **Haunted** or **Silenced**, you may reduce the duration by one step (10 min→1 min→1 round→ends after your turn).

- **Hushblade Rapier** *(Rapier)* — Tags: REGION:VAROOK  
  **Hook:** Advantage on **Insight** checks to detect manipulation, false vows, or memory pressure (One-Modifier Rule applies).

## Light Pet regions (tools that feel “helpful”)
- **Gaianok Bramble Spear** *(Spear)* — Tags: REGION:VERDANT_VALLEY, SPIRIT:LIGHT  
  **Hook (1/Scene):** If you use an action to protect an ally (interpose, shove to safety), grant that ally +1 to its next Procedure Check this scene (does not stack).

- **Shakoran Tide Trident** *(Trident)* — Tags: REGION:WATERS, SPIRIT:LIGHT  
  **Hook (1/Short Rest):** As an action, splash/cleanse: grant advantage on one save vs poison/disease this scene (ally you can reach).

- **Keairvari Windglaive** *(Glaive)* — Tags: REGION:SKYWARD_PEAKS, SPIRIT:LIGHT  
  **Hook:** You can signal across distance with chimes/flags mounted on the haft; allies who see the signal gain +1 on their next navigation Procedure Check (no stacking).

- **Nyvakira Emberchain** *(Flail)* — Tags: REGION:EMBERWOOD, SPIRIT:LIGHT  
  **Hook (1/Long Rest):** When you would gain **Minor Strain** from heat or a FIRE technique, you may ignore it (represents venting heat through chain rhythm).

# 4) Implements (focus items that complement spells + techniques)
Implements work as **spellcasting focuses** (as appropriate) and also act as *technique anchors* for Calanthor.

## Universal implements
- **Rite Chalk & Cord** — boundary marking, treaty rites, witness lines  
- **Traveler’s Kit** — rope, pitons, cloth mask, ash pouch, tinder

## Element implements (common)
- **Wind Chimes Harness (AIR)** — navigation, signaling, “tell” clarity  
- **Water Sash (WATER)** — cleansing, binding practice  
- **Ember Rod (FIRE)** — ignition control, heat discipline  
- **Stone Gauntlets (EARTH)** — bracing, stance training  
- **Seed Pouch & Shears (VERDANT)** — mutualist rites, antidote prep  
- **Rime Beads (ICE)** — breath counting, whiteout orientation

**Implement hook rule:** Implements may grant advantage on **one specific Procedure Check category** 1/scene (choose at purchase/crafting). No combat bonuses.

# 5) Generator fields (minimum)
Weapons should store:
- `id`, `name`, `base_profile`
- `category`, `damage_die`, `damage_type`, `properties`
- `tags` (region/culture/spirit)
- `resonance_hook` (frequency + effect) (optional)

Implements should store:
- `id`, `name`, `element`, `use_as_focus` (yes/no), `procedure_hook`
