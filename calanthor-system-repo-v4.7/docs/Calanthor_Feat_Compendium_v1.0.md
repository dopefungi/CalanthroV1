# Calanthor Feat Compendium (v1.0)
*Three feat buckets: Discipline Expansions, Culture Feats, and Overlay Feats—balanced and lore-congruent.*

This compendium adds feats in three “logical places”:
1) **Technique-feat expansions** (deepen an elemental discipline without adding raw damage creep)
2) **Culture feats** (represent rites, legal standing, and survival training)
3) **Overlay feats** (rare, story-earned marks that enhance counterplay with domain pressure)

All feats are written to stay compatible with standard 5e character building.

---

## Global balance rules (official)
### One-Modifier Rule (restate)
On any single roll, you may apply only **one** of:
- Culture Gift (background)
- Class Edge
- Region Resonance/Attunement passive
- Culture feat “boost” (if it offers +PB or advantage)

(Unless the GM explicitly allows a climactic exception.)

### No combat-math creep
Unless stated otherwise, feats in this document do **not**:
- increase weapon damage
- increase spell damage
- increase AC
- grant blanket resistances

They instead add:
- techniques (already balanced by uses/DCs)
- procedure counterplay
- social/legal access
- narrow, scene-based utility

# Bucket 1: Technique-Feat Expansions (v1.0)
These feats require **Elemental Discipline Initiate** in the listed discipline.
They are designed to:
- feel like specialized bending training
- add one signature technique choice + one small “handle”
- avoid becoming “free spells”

> **Note:** You already have the core ladder feats (Initiate/Adept/Master) in `Calanthor_Elemental_Discipline_Feats_v1.0.md`.
> These are *optional specialization feats*.

## FEAT: Ember Specialist (FIRE)
**Prerequisite:** Elemental Discipline Initiate (FIRE), level 4+

- Learn **one additional FIRE technique** you qualify for (Novice; Adept if level 5+).
- **Heat Budget:** Once per long rest, when you would gain **Minor Strain** from your own FIRE technique or environmental heat exposure, you may ignore that Minor Strain.

## FEAT: Gale Specialist (AIR)
**Prerequisite:** Elemental Discipline Initiate (AIR), level 4+

- Learn **one additional AIR technique** you qualify for (Novice; Adept if level 5+).
- **Wind Footing:** Once per short rest, you may ignore difficult terrain caused by wind, loose scree, or unstable ridge for one turn (no action).

## FEAT: Tide Specialist (WATER)
**Prerequisite:** Elemental Discipline Initiate (WATER), level 4+

- Learn **one additional WATER technique** you qualify for (Novice; Adept if level 5+).
- **Clean Hands:** Once per long rest, when you end **poisoned** (nonmagical) or **Minor Strain** using a WATER technique, you may also grant the target **+PB** to its next saving throw this scene (choose one save).

## FEAT: Winter Specialist (ICE)
**Prerequisite:** Elemental Discipline Initiate (ICE), level 4+

- Learn **one additional ICE technique** you qualify for (Novice; Adept if level 5+).
- **Cold Accounting:** Once per long rest, when you would become **Chilled**, you may instead gain **Minor Strain** (your choice; represents controlled breathing).

## FEAT: Stone Specialist (EARTH)
**Prerequisite:** Elemental Discipline Initiate (EARTH), level 4+

- Learn **one additional EARTH technique** you qualify for (Novice; Adept if level 5+).
- **Bracework:** Once per short rest, when you or an ally within 10 ft would be shoved or knocked prone, you may reduce the forced movement by 10 ft or prevent prone (no action).

## FEAT: Bloom Specialist (VERDANT)
**Prerequisite:** Elemental Discipline Initiate (VERDANT), level 4+

- Learn **one additional VERDANT technique** you qualify for (Novice; Adept if level 5+).
- **Field Botanist:** Once per long rest, you may treat a failed **Nature** or **Medicine** check made to identify toxins/spores as a success with cost (GM applies a minor cost).

# Bucket 2: Culture Feats (v1.0)
Culture feats represent deeper training, rites, and standing. They are usually taken by characters with the related culture background, but the GM may allow training access in play.

## FEAT: Cairn-Wright (Frostborn)
**Prerequisite:** Frostborn Whisperer background (or GM training in Kaldraun)

- Gain proficiency with **Mason’s tools** or **Cartographer’s tools** (choose one).
- **Cairn Sign:** You can create or interpret cairn marks. Once per travel leg, you can reduce a travel obstacle’s DC by **2** (representing correct route marking).
- **Shelter Craft:** When you take a short rest in cold terrain, your party may treat improvised shelter as “proper shelter” once per day (prevents one cold-exposure consequence).

## FEAT: Oath-Brand Adept (Ashwalker)
**Prerequisite:** Ashwalker Vow-Bearer background (or GM training in Khalsarra)

- Gain proficiency in **Persuasion** or **Intimidation** (choose one).
- **Witnessed Vow:** Once per long rest, as an action you may declare a vow tied to the current obstacle. Until the obstacle ends, you have advantage on one check directly pursuing that vow (choose when you roll).
- **Mercy Clause:** When you offer mercy/restitution sincerely, you gain advantage on one related social check this scene (GM discretion).

## FEAT: River-Law Advocate (Shakoran)
**Prerequisite:** Shakoran Mediator background (or GM training in Waters)

- Gain proficiency with **Calligrapher’s supplies** or **Ritecraft** (choose one).
- **Treaty Form:** Once per long rest, you can force a hearing before violence in a rights dispute unless a taboo has been broken (GM may impose time pressure or concession).
- **Neutral Boundary:** Once per scene, you can create a “neutral space” agreement; creatures who accept it have disadvantage on the first hostile action they take that scene (a social lock, not magical compulsion).

## FEAT: Steward of Mutualism (Gaianok)
**Prerequisite:** Gaianok Steward background (or GM training in Verdant Valley)

- Gain proficiency in **Animal Handling** or **Survival** (choose one).
- **Restitution Sense:** Once per scene, you can identify the “least harmful” approach to a problem (GM provides one concrete, low-harm option).
- **Seed Cache:** Once per long rest, you can create a basic remedy bundle; one creature gains advantage on its next CON save vs poison/disease this day.

## FEAT: Ember Rite Savant (Nyvakira)
**Prerequisite:** Emberwood Rite-Dancer background (or GM training in Emberwood)

- Gain proficiency with **Smith’s tools** or **Ritecraft** (choose one).
- **Vent the Flare:** Once per scene, you can stabilize a heat hazard or panic flare—reduce the DC of the next related Procedure Check by **2**.
- **Ash Hospitality:** You can always find a safe place to make fire in civilized areas unless explicitly forbidden (GM discretion).

## FEAT: Wind-Writ Courier (Keairvari)
**Prerequisite:** Aeris Sky-Sentinel background (or GM training in Skyward Peaks)

- Gain proficiency with **Navigator’s tools** (if you lack it).
- **Route Omens:** Once per travel leg, you may reroll one Survival/Perception check made to navigate storms or ridges (must take new roll).
- **Signal Chimes:** You can signal across long distances with chimes/flags; allies who can see/hear you gain +1 to their next Procedure Check this scene (does not stack with other boosts).

# Bucket 3: Overlay Feats (v1.0)
Overlay feats are **rare** and should usually be awarded through story:
- a bargain
- a rite
- surviving a Cataclysm
- being witnessed by a Great Pet’s agent

They do not make overlays “free.” They improve **counterplay** and reduce frustration.

## FEAT: Mark of Kaldraun (Stasis-Bound)
**Prerequisite:** GM-awarded (Kaldraun rite) or surviving a Kaldraun Cataclysm

- **Cold Witness:** You have advantage on saves against **Chilled**.
- **Brace the Veil (1/Long Rest):** When a winter hazard would tick the KALDRAUN_STASIS track (or apply a track 2/4 effect), you may prevent that tick/effect for your party for that obstacle (the hazard still exists; you found the one safe line).

## FEAT: Brand of Khalsarra (Weight-Witness)
**Prerequisite:** GM-awarded (oath rite) or completing a vow trial in Cracked Earth

- **Purpose Anchor:** You have advantage on saves against **Weighed**.
- **Declare Purpose (1/Long Rest):** As an action, declare a purpose tied to the current scene. Reduce the KHALSARRA_WEIGHT track by **1** (minimum 0) or prevent one tick this obstacle (GM choice based on fiction).

## FEAT: Charnelix Scar (Blight-Touched)
**Prerequisite:** GM-awarded (survived blightwell exposure)

- **Spore Sense:** You have advantage on checks to detect contamination/spores.
- **Burn the Blight (1/Long Rest):** When a creature would become **Blighted**, you may instead give it **Minor Strain** (your choice) and end one “spore zone” effect in a 10-ft radius (if present).

## FEAT: Varook’s Witness (Hush-Anchored)
**Prerequisite:** GM-awarded (witness rite in Varook domain)

- **Name Anchor:** You have advantage on saves against **Haunted**.
- **Hold the Line (1/Long Rest):** When a hush/memory effect would impose **Silenced** or **Haunted** on your party, choose up to PB creatures; they ignore that condition this time (the scene still changes—this is a shield, not immunity).

---

## Implementation note
Overlay feats should never stack to create permanent immunity. If a player collects multiple overlay feats, the GM may require:
- increased duties/omens
- rival attention
- narrative costs

That keeps them mythic.
