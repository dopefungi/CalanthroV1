# Calanthor: First Adventure — *The Frostcrack Witness* (v1.0)
*A level 1–3 starter adventure for the Kaldraun–Khalsarra border.*

**Tone:** harsh travel, vows and witnesses, a small mystery, one tight fight, one “set piece” fight.  
**Domains in play:** **KALDRAUN_STASIS** (whiteout/stasis) and **KHALSARRA_WEIGHT** (burden/vow).  
**Session length:** 1 long session or 2 shorter sessions.

---

## Premise
A treaty courier carrying a **Witness Seal** vanished at the **Frostcrack Causeway**, the only safe route between cracked basalt ridges and the tundra driftline.
Both sides blame the other. If the seal is lost, the next caravan will be turned back—possibly violently.

The PCs are hired (or obligated) to:
1) retrieve the courier (or their seal),
2) discover what happened,
3) prevent the border from tipping into conflict.

---

## What the GM needs
- Social framework (Disposition track)
- Exploration procedure (watches, SU, obstacles)
- Domain overlays (track 2/4/6)
- Starter Bestiary (or on-the-spot builder)

Recommended monsters used (from Starter Bestiary):
- **Driftglass Whelp (CR 1)** — scouting pressure in whiteout
- **Dustbound Skirmisher (CR 3)** — terrain control at the causeway

---

## Adventure structure (3 acts)
### Act I — The Gate of Witnesses (Social)
### Act II — The Frostcrack Approach (Exploration)
### Act III — The Causeway Break (Encounter + Resolution)

---

# Act I — The Gate of Witnesses
**Scene:** Border checkpoint at **Stone‑and‑Snow Gate**.
A Khalsarran clerk and a Frostborn watch captain argue in front of the caravan line.

### Objective
Secure legal passage to search the causeway and claim the seal in the name of the treaty.

### Disposition track (start 2: guarded/listening)
- **Track 2:** They demand a test: a vow, a fee, or proof of competence.
- **Track 4:** They reveal their fear: if the seal is forged, the wrong faction wins.
- **Track 6:** They grant a pass and a witness token (or escort).

### Key NPCs
- **Captain Vaska Driftmark (Frostborn):** wants order; fears a stasis omen. (WIS/Insight heavy)
- **Clerk Harun Oathscribe (Khalsarra):** wants precedent; hates improvisation. (History/Ritecraft heavy)
- **Caravan Speaker Nemi:** wants speed; will offer coin but not delays.

### Leverage options
- Show a lawful token (Shakoran law seal, treaty papers).
- Use **Rite Chalk & Cord** to establish a witness line.
- Cite precedent (History) about courier protection clauses.
- Offer a vow (“We return the seal to the witness circle, untouched.”)

### Fail-forward costs (pick 1 on a miss)
- You get the pass but must accept an escort (complication).
- You get the pass but owe a favor (**Owed** tag).
- You get the pass but start with overlay track +1 (pressure rises).

---

# Act II — The Frostcrack Approach (Exploration)
**Travel:** 1 watch from gate to the causeway ridge.

### Supplies
Start the party at **6 SU** for the adventure (adjust up/down for campaign).
Each watch costs 1 SU.

### Obstacle 1 (choose 1)
1) **Whiteout Wall (Kaldraun)** — Survival DC 13 (Navigator).  
   - Success: no delay; prevent 1 overlay tick.
   - Fail-forward: still reach the ridge, but lose 1 SU or gain Minor Strain.
2) **Fault Rattle (Khalsarra)** — Athletics or Survival DC 13.  
   - Success: safe path across loose basalt.
   - Fail-forward: lose time (extra turn) and start Act III with overlay +1.

### Encounter check (optional)
On a 1 on 1d6, a **Driftglass Whelp** circles the party in the fog:
- it tests weakness, then flees if bloodied
- its purpose is to foreshadow counterplay (“torch light matters”)

If you want no combat here, show signs: paw-prints, rime cracks, distant eyes.

---

# Act III — The Causeway Break
The Frostcrack Causeway is a narrow basalt bridge over a frozen ravine.
Half of it has collapsed. The courier’s sled is below—half buried in snow and stone dust.

### The truth (GM)
A **Dustbound Skirmisher** (Khalsarra) ambushed the courier to seize the seal… but was then hunted by a tundra omen.
They hid the seal inside a cairn to “let the mountains judge.” Now both domains are escalating.

### Site actions (10-minute turns)
- **Search the wreck** (Investigation DC 13): find courier’s journal + clue about a “stone chant”.
- **Treat the courier (if alive)** (Medicine DC 13): stabilize; learn they were promised safe passage.
- **Read the cairn marks** (Ritecraft or History DC 13): reveals where the seal is hidden.
- **Make a safe lane** (Athletics/Tools DC 13): creates a stable route for the party.

Each failure should still find progress, with a cost (time/SU/Minor Strain/overlay tick).

---

## Set piece encounter: “The Bridge Holds, Until It Doesn’t”
**Trigger:** When the PCs attempt to take the seal or cross the bridge with it.

### Enemies (pick based on level)
**Level 1 party (easier):**
- 1× **Dustbound Skirmisher** (use as CR 2 by reducing HP and removing one terrain trick)
- 1× **Driftglass Whelp** (arrives on round 3)

**Level 2–3 party (default):**
- 1× **Dustbound Skirmisher** as written (CR 3)
- 1× **Driftglass Whelp** as written (CR 1)

**Hard mode / mini‑boss:**
- Make the skirmisher **Elite** (Elite template) and add **Oathstone Burden** threshold package.

### Terrain features (make the fight Calanthor)
- **Loose basalt**: difficult terrain in 2 squares
- **Whiteout pocket**: lightly obscured 10-ft patch (torch reduces it for 1 round)
- **Ravine edge**: forced movement risks a fall (but include clear tells)

### Overlay interaction (use lightly)
Start both overlay tracks at **1**.
- When a PC fails a key save/check, tick the relevant track.
- When the party uses smart counterplay (torch line, witness chalk, bracing), reduce or prevent a tick.

### Enemy counterplay reminders (say them out loud)
- Torch/bright line suppresses whiteout tricks (Kaldraun counterplay).
- Witness chalk boundaries help resist weight pressure (Khalsarra counterplay).
- Breaking/clearing difficult terrain creates openings.

### Victory conditions
- Win the fight **or** force a retreat with the seal **or** negotiate mid-fight using a vow (great Calanthor moment).

---

## Resolution (choose ending)
### Ending A — “Seal Returned, Border Calms”
They return the seal and establish a witness line; both sides grant a favor.

### Ending B — “Seal Claimed, But a Debt Is Owed”
They keep the seal for a higher authority. Gain **Owed** to one faction and **Suspected** to the other.

### Ending C — “The Mountains Judge”
They place the seal in a cairn and vow to deliver judgment later. Overlay pressure reduces, but the political clock begins.

## Rewards (Calanthor-style, non power-creep)
- **Witness Token** (lets you establish a witness line once/day; narrative leverage)
- **Clanwork weapon** from the region (tags + narrow procedure help)
- **Materials**: driftglass shard, oathstone chip (crafting components)
- Optional (if level 3+): 1 **Ritebound** item with 1 hook + attached debt
