# Calanthor Lineages & Culture Backgrounds — Official Pack (v1.0)
*Canon-ready trait blocks and background templates for generator-safe character creation.*

This pack formalizes:
- **2 Calanthor-native lineages** (Aeris, Emberkin)
- **6 core culture backgrounds** (Frostborn, Ashwalker, Shakoran, Gaianok, Emberwood, Aeris)
- **2 threshold backgrounds** (Mire Marshrunner, Gloomward Witness)

It follows the Calanthor framework:
- **Species/Lineage = physiology**
- **Culture = background**
- **Region Attunement (optional) = resonance package** (already in region packs)

---

## 1) Official Lineage: Aeris (Skyborn)
**Lore:** Wind-adapted people of the Skyward Peaks who practice route-omens and vow-bound air rites.

### Aeris Traits
- **Creature Type:** Humanoid  
- **Size:** Medium or Small (choose when you select this lineage)  
- **Speed:** 30 ft  
- **Wind Sense:** Choose one proficiency: **Perception** or **Survival**  
- **Ritecraft:** You gain proficiency with **Ritecraft tools**  
- **Skyborn Physiology:** You gain **Tier 0 (Jump + Glide)** from the Calanthor Flight rules at 1st level.  
- **Aerial Mastery Unlocks:** You unlock higher tiers automatically at character levels **5 / 9 / 13 / 17** (Tier 1–4).  
- **Armor Limits:** While wearing **heavy armor**, you cannot use Tier 1+ flight features. While wearing **medium armor**, each scene you fly you gain **+1 Aerial Strain**.

**Flight Reference:** See `docs/Calanthor_Flight_Official_Options_v1.0.md` for full tier/strain rules and DC tables.

### Design notes (GM-facing)
- Aeris are excellent scouts and vertical movers at low levels but do not bypass walls/encounters.
- The Aerial Strain track is the fairness lever in storms, whiteouts, spore clouds, and mythic pressure.

## 2) Official Lineage: Emberkin (Cinder-Blooded)
**Lore:** Emberwood-adapted people who survive by heatcraft, renewal rites, and ash ecology.

### Emberkin Traits
- **Creature Type:** Humanoid  
- **Size:** Medium (or Small if your table allows)  
- **Speed:** 30 ft  
- **Heat-Hardened:** You have **resistance to fire damage**. In addition, you automatically succeed on saving throws against the *environmental* effects of extreme heat (DMG-style) unless the effect is magical or a Great Pet event.  
- **Ash-Sight:** You can see normally through nonmagical smoke/ash out to **30 ft** (this does not negate heavily obscured beyond that range).  
- **Ritecraft:** You gain proficiency with **Ritecraft tools**.  
- **Renewal Spark (1/Long Rest):** When you are reduced to 0 hit points but not killed outright, you can drop to **1 hit point instead**. When you do, you gain **Minor Strain**.

### Design notes (GM-facing)
- Renewal Spark is intentionally similar in power to common “refuse to drop” lineage traits.
- Fire resistance is strong; avoid stacking multiple resistances at low levels in your campaign.

# Culture Backgrounds (Official)
All Calanthor culture backgrounds include:
- 2 skill proficiencies
- 1 tool proficiency (often Ritecraft)
- 1 language (or a tool/vehicle swap)
- background feature (access/rights/contacts)
- **Cultural Gift (1/scene):** add **+PB** to a fitting check (does not stack with Region Attunement passive on the same roll unless GM allows)

## 3) Frostborn Whisperer (Kaldraun)
- **Skills:** Survival, Perception  
- **Tools:** Ritecraft  
- **Language:** One of your choice  
- **Equipment:** Cold-weather gear, a travel journal or slate, tinderbox, 10 days rations, waterskin, 10 gp  

**Feature — Cairn Rights:** In Frostborn territories, you recognize cairn-marks and shelter claims. Once per travel leg, you can request safe shelter or guidance from a cairn-keeper or Frostborn band. They may ask a duty (watch rotation, message, shared warmth rite) in return.

**Cultural Gift (1/scene) — Read the Cracks:** Add +PB to a check to interpret ice fractures, aurora omens, or whiteout routes.

## 4) Ashwalker Vow-Bearer (Khalsarra)
- **Skills:** Insight, Survival  
- **Tools:** Ritecraft  
- **Language:** One of your choice  
- **Equipment:** Traveler’s clothes, a vow token (stone/cord), incense or ash-chalk, bedroll, 10 days rations, waterskin, 10 gp  

**Feature — Oath-Witness:** In Khalsarra lands, communities and nomad circles will hear a vow sworn in good faith. If the vow aligns with local justice, they will offer one practical aid (supplies, escort to a waypoint, safe resting circle) once per significant arc—then expect follow-through.

**Cultural Gift (1/scene) — Declare Purpose:** Add +PB to a check made to resist despair, endure hardship, or negotiate mercy in a charged place.

## 5) Shakoran Mediator (Waters)
- **Skills:** Persuasion, Insight  
- **Tools:** Choose one: Ritecraft (Purification kit) or Calligrapher’s supplies  
- **Language:** One of your choice  
- **Equipment:** Fine cloak or waterproof mantle, purification tokens or ink set, a seal of river-law, 10 days rations, waterskin, 10 gp  

**Feature — River-Law Hearing:** You know water-right customs and treaty forms. When conflict is about passage, tribute, or rights, you can usually secure a hearing before violence—unless you (or allies) have violated a clear taboo.

**Cultural Gift (1/scene) — Clean Hands:** Add +PB to a check to cleanse contamination, de-escalate conflict, or establish neutral ground.

## 6) Gaianok Steward (Verdant Valley)
- **Skills:** Nature, Medicine  
- **Tools:** Choose one: Herbalism kit or Ritecraft  
- **Language:** One of your choice  
- **Equipment:** Herbalism pouch or rite bundle, a steward’s sash/token, 10 days rations, waterskin, 10 gp  

**Feature — Steward’s Claim:** Steward circles recognize your role. You can request basic lodging, seeds, or remedies from a steward site if you pledge restitution (labor, protection, replanting, mediation).

**Cultural Gift (1/scene) — Mutualism:** Add +PB to a check to calm beasts, identify safe plants, or prevent ecological harm.

## 7) Emberwood Rite-Dancer (Nyvakira)
- **Skills:** Athletics, and choose one: Intimidation or Performance  
- **Tools:** Ritecraft  
- **Language:** One of your choice  
- **Equipment:** Fire-safe wraps, a ritual ember-charm, flint/steel, 10 days rations, waterskin, 10 gp  

**Feature — Ember Hospitality:** Emberwood enclaves will offer shelter and guidance if you respect renewal rites. If you break a rite taboo, this protection is withdrawn until you make restitution.

**Cultural Gift (1/scene) — Vent the Heat:** Add +PB to a check to cross heat hazards, stabilize a flare-up, or craft safe fire.

## 8) Aeris Sky-Sentinel (Keairvari)
- **Skills:** Acrobatics, Perception  
- **Tools:** Choose one: Navigator’s tools or Ritecraft  
- **Language:** One of your choice  
- **Equipment:** Navigator’s kit or rite kit, wind-chimes or a vow cord, 10 days rations, waterskin, 10 gp  

**Feature — Wind-Writ Access:** Aeris outposts will share route omens, storm warnings, or ridge paths if you offer truthful news or service (scouting, courier work, oath-keeping).

**Cultural Gift (1/scene) — Wind-Read:** Add +PB to a check to predict storms, find a safe ridge path, or time a traversal.

# Threshold Backgrounds (Optional)
These represent border peoples who survive “near” the uninhabitable zones.

## 9) Mire Marshrunner (Charnelix border)
- **Skills:** Stealth, Medicine  
- **Tools:** Choose one: Poisoner’s kit or Herbalism kit  
- **Language:** One of your choice  
- **Equipment:** Waders or stilt wraps, a field antitoxin kit, 10 days rations, waterskin, 10 gp  

**Feature — Stilt-Camp Routes:** You know two safe crossings (GM places them) across an outer-mire web-lane or stilt trail.

**Cultural Gift (1/scene) — Field Antitoxin:** Add +PB to a check to mitigate spores/poison or to stabilize contaminated wounds (this does not replace full cures).

## 10) Gloomward Witness (Varook border)
- **Skills:** Religion, Insight  
- **Tools:** Ritecraft  
- **Language:** One of your choice  
- **Equipment:** Chalk/charcoal and cord, a witness token, 10 days rations, waterskin, 10 gp  

**Feature — Witness Rite:** During a short rest, you can establish a basic name-ward circle for your party. Until the next rest, the first time a party member would gain **Haunted**, they may ignore it.

**Cultural Gift (1/scene) — Anchor the Name:** Add +PB to a check to resist fear/memory manipulation, or to recall a critical fact under pressure.

---

## 11) Balance rule (official)
If a **Cultural Gift** and a **Region Attunement passive** would apply to the same roll, choose **one** (no stacking) unless the GM explicitly allows it.
