# Calanthor Condition Appendix (v1.0)
*A complete, table-ready condition list for Calanthor (D&D-dominant), including Calanthor-unique conditions and how they interact with Procedure Checks, Domain Overlays, and Counterplay.*

**Design goals**
- Readable like 5e
- Harsh domains feel dangerous, but **never “turn off” play**
- Conditions should usually be **short**, **clear**, and have **counterplay**

---

## 0) Condition rules (official)
### 0.1 Categories
Calanthor uses three categories of “state”:
1) **Combat Conditions** (mechanical, usually short)
2) **Exploration Conditions** (fatigue, exposure, contamination)
3) **Social Tags** (reputation/standing; not combat conditions)

This appendix covers (1) and (2) fully, and summarizes (3) for completeness.

### 0.2 Stacking and escalation
Unless a condition says otherwise:
- The same condition **does not stack**.
- If it would be applied again, **refresh duration** *or* **escalate** if the condition has an escalation step.

### 0.3 Duration default
If a feature doesn’t specify duration, use these defaults:
- **Combat:** until the end of the target’s next turn
- **Exploration:** until a short rest *or* proper counterplay is applied
- **Social Tags:** until resolved fictionally (pay debt, clear suspicion, etc.)

### 0.4 The No Shut‑Down rule (conditions edition)
Conditions may pressure choices, but should not remove a character’s ability to act for long stretches.
If a condition would fully lock someone down (e.g., sustained silence, permanent restraint), the GM must provide:
- a **tell**
- a **counterplay option** (resource/skill/choice)

### 0.5 Removal priority
When multiple effects could remove/mitigate conditions:
1) **Counterplay** (on monster blocks / domains) is fastest
2) **Tools/gear** (mask, heat, chalk, clean water)
3) **Skills** (Medicine, Ritecraft, Survival)
4) **Rest** (short/long)

# 1) Core D&D Conditions (Calanthor uses standard text)
Calanthor uses the standard D&D conditions as written:
- Blinded
- Charmed
- Deafened
- Frightened
- Grappled
- Incapacitated
- Invisible
- Paralyzed
- Petrified
- Poisoned
- Prone
- Restrained
- Stunned
- Unconscious
- Exhaustion (see Calanthor note below)

### 1.1 Exhaustion (recommended Calanthor handling)
You may use standard 5e exhaustion or a simplified approach:
- **Exhaustion 1–2**: treat as “Minor Strain escalation” (see below)
- **Exhaustion 3+**: serious; apply standard exhaustion effects

# 2) Calanthor Core Conditions (new)
These are the “must-have” Calanthor conditions referenced across domains, monsters, and travel.

## 2.1 Minor Strain (Exploration/Combat)
**What it is:** stress, fatigue, breath burn, spiritual pressure—Calanthor’s “soft currency” for consequences.  
**Effect:** none by itself, but it escalates into harsher states and fuels fail-forward costs.

**Rules**
- Track Minor Strain as a tally (0+).
- **At 3 Strain:** the character becomes **Strained** (see 2.2).
- **At 6 Strain:** the character becomes **Worn** (see 2.3).
- Gaining Strain beyond 6 increases narrative risk; the GM should push rest or concessions.

**Removal**
- Short rest: remove **1** Minor Strain (if safe).
- Long rest: remove **all** Minor Strain (unless the domain says otherwise).
- Certain gear/rituals can remove 1/scene (rare).

> GM guidance: Minor Strain is your default cost instead of “nothing happens.”

## 2.2 Strained (Escalation state)
**Trigger:** 3 Minor Strain.  
**Effect:** Once per scene, the GM may impose **disadvantage on one roll** you make that is directly tied to the cause of strain (cold, blight, hush, burden). After it triggers, it can’t trigger again until you gain more strain or the scene ends.

**Removal**
- Reduce Minor Strain below 3 OR complete a short rest in safety.

## 2.3 Worn (Escalation state)
**Trigger:** 6 Minor Strain.  
**Effect:** Your **speed is reduced by 10**, and you cannot benefit from “Fast pace” travel. Also, you have disadvantage on **Constitution saves** vs environmental hazards.

**Removal**
- Reduce Minor Strain below 6 AND take a long rest in safety.

---

## 2.4 Chilled (Kaldraun / cold / ice)
**Effect**
- Your **speed is reduced by 10**.
- The first time you make a Strength or Dexterity check on your turn, you do so at **disadvantage** (frozen joints).

**Duration**
- Combat: until end of your next turn.
- Exploration: until you gain heat/shelter OR short rest.

**Escalation**
- If you are Chilled and would gain Chilled again, you become **Chilled (Deep)** until end of your next turn:
  - speed -20 instead of -10
  - you can’t take reactions this round (stiffness)

**Removal / Counterplay**
- **Heat & Shelter** resource, proper cold gear, or fire line reduces Chilled by one step (Deep→normal→removed).
- **Breath Discipline** (CON/Survival action) lets you ignore Chilled penalties for 1 round on success.

---

## 2.5 Weighed (Khalsarra / burden / oathstone)
**Effect**
- Your **speed is reduced by 10**.
- You have **disadvantage on Athletics and Acrobatics** checks made to jump, climb, or resist terrain effects.

**Duration**
- Combat: until end of your next turn.
- Exploration: until you rest or complete a relevant rite.

**Escalation**
- If Weighed is applied while you are already Weighed, you become **Anchored** until end of your next turn (see 2.6).

**Removal / Counterplay**
- **Witness Chalk** boundary: advantage on saves to resist being Weighed for 1 round.
- **Declare Purpose** (vow + act) can reduce Weighed by one step (GM adjudicates once/scene).

---

## 2.6 Anchored (Khalsarra / earth control)
**Effect**
- Your speed becomes **0** until end of your next turn.
- You can still act, but you **can’t take reactions**.

**Removal**
- Ends at end of your next turn automatically.
- **Break the Anchor** (Athletics/Ritecraft action) can end Anchored early on success.
- Forced movement immunity doesn’t prevent Anchored (it’s not a push)—it’s a lock.

> Note: Anchored is intentionally short. It’s a “moment of terror,” not a permanent stun.

---

## 2.7 Blighted (Charnelix / disease / rot pressure)
**Effect**
- You have disadvantage on **Constitution saves** against poison, disease, and contamination procedures.
- When you regain HP from a spell or feature, reduce the healing by **1 per spell level** (or by **1** if non‑spell).

**Duration**
- Exploration: until treated with clean water/fire/medicine.
- Combat: usually 1 minute unless specified otherwise.

**Escalation**
- If Blighted is applied again while already Blighted, you also gain **1 Minor Strain** (contamination spreads).

**Removal / Counterplay**
- **Clean Water / Fire** removes Blighted (Track 4+: may require a CON save per your counterplay template).
- **Medicine** antidote craft can grant advantage vs further blight this scene.

---

## 2.8 Haunted (Varook / memory warfare)
**Effect**
- You have disadvantage on **Insight** and **Perception** checks to read intent or detect deception/illusions.
- The first time each scene you fail a Wisdom save, you take **1 Minor Strain** (psychological backlash).

**Duration**
- 10 minutes by default (or as specified).

**Removal / Counterplay**
- **Recall Anchor** (Insight/History action) reduces duration by one step.
- **Speak the Truth** (choice/bargain) can clear Haunted immediately at a story cost (GM).

---

## 2.9 Silenced (Varook / air pressure / hush)
**Effect**
- You can’t cast spells with **verbal components**.
- You can still communicate normally (unless a feature says “Muted”).

**Duration**
- Until end of your next turn unless specified otherwise.

**Removal / Counterplay**
- **Name-Cord** or **Recall Anchor** can shorten/negate one application as per their templates.

> If you want a harsher version later, make it a separate condition: **Muted** (rare).

---

## 2.10 Marked (Spirit Omen: Light/Dark)
**Effect**
- You carry a visible or sensed omen of **SPIRIT:LIGHT** or **SPIRIT:DARK**.
- Mechanical impact is **social**, not combat: courts and circles treat you differently.

**Use**
- Apply Marked as a consequence of bargains, relic use, or Track 6 events.
- Marked grants leverage to those who recognize it, and may apply **Suspected** or **Trusted** tags depending on the faction.

**Removal**
- A rite of clarification, a witness circle, or story resolution.

# 3) Environmental “Zones” (not conditions)
These are common in monster grafts and overlays. They are **terrain states**, not personal conditions.
Examples:
- **Whiteout Pocket:** lightly obscured; navigation harder; torches reduce it 1 round.
- **Anchor Field:** difficult terrain; may prevent Dash; Break the Anchor can clear a square.
- **Spore Zone:** entering/starting forces a CON save vs Minor Strain or Blighted depending on track.
- **Cinder Line:** difficult terrain + small fire damage; Smother & Starve clears it 1 round.

**Rule:** Zones always come with at least one counterplay option (resource/skill/choice).

---

# 4) Social Tags (reference)
Tags are not combat conditions; they influence DCs, leverage, and NPC behavior.
- **Witnessed:** recorded; harder to deny
- **Owed:** debt/favor outstanding
- **Suspected:** watched; Deception DC +2 vs that group
- **Trusted:** Persuasion DC -2 vs that group
- **Bound by Vow:** breaking it has real consequences (Owed/Marked/overlay tick)

---

# 5) GM quick application guide
When you need to pick a condition quickly, use this:
- Cold/whiteout → **Chilled**
- Burden/stone rites → **Weighed** (escalate to **Anchored**)
- Mire/rot/disease → **Blighted**
- Hush/memory war → **Haunted** or **Silenced**
- Generic stress/cost → **Minor Strain** (escalate to Strained/Worn)

If the table is already under pressure, prefer **Minor Strain** over hard control.

---

# 6) Condition formatting for statblocks (standard)
When writing monsters/techniques, format like:
- **Condition:** Name (duration) — effect
- **Removal:** how to end it
- **Counterplay:** one actionable method

Example:
**Chilled (until end of next turn):** speed -10; first STR/DEX check each turn at disadvantage.  
**Removal:** heat/shelter reduces by one step.  
**Counterplay:** Breath Discipline (action) ignore penalties 1 round.
