# Calanthor Exploration & Travel Procedure (v1.0)
*Exploration that feels like Calanthor: harsh domains, overlay pressure, and fail-forward travel obstacles.*

This module is built to work with:
- **Procedure Check Usage Rules**
- **Domain Overlay Operating Rules**
- **Equipment kits + implements**
- **Monster Gen** (encounters as pressure releases)

---

## 1) Time units (official)
Use whichever your table prefers; this system supports both.

### 1.1 The Watch (overland)
A **Watch** is ~4 hours of travel/work.
Typical day: 3 watches (morning / midday / night).

### 1.2 The Turn (site exploration)
A **Turn** is ~10 minutes in a ruin, cavern, or hostile site.
Resting 1 hour = 6 turns.

---

## 2) Party Roles (pick each watch/turn)
Assign roles; a PC can hold 1 role at a time.
- **Navigator** (Survival / tools): route, pace, avoid hazards
- **Scout** (Stealth / Perception): spot threats, avoid ambushes
- **Lookout** (Perception): watch skies/lines, early warning
- **Quartermaster** (Survival / Investigation): supplies, shelter, ration logic
- **Ritualist/Witness** (Ritecraft): ward lines, safe camps, treaty rites

If nobody takes a role, the GM may apply disadvantage or advance overlay track faster.

---

## 3) Travel Pace (per watch)
- **Steady:** baseline; normal checks
- **Fast:** +1 “distance step” but Scout checks are harder; more risk
- **Careful:** -1 distance step but advantage on one hazard check (GM picks)

Distance steps are setting-scale; pick what works:
- 1 step = 6–12 miles (road) or 3–6 miles (wilderness)

---

## 4) Exploration as Obstacles (the Calanthor loop)
Each watch/turn, the GM frames an **Obstacle**:
- hazard, weather, navigation, social border, spirit omen, etc.

Run it as:
1) **Declare stakes** (what happens on fail-forward)
2) **Assign check** (Navigator/Scout/etc.)
3) **Roll** → apply result using Procedure rules
4) **Track pressure** (domain overlays, supplies, fatigue)

### Typical fail-forward costs
- lose time (extra turn/watch)
- lose supplies (rations, water, kit charges)
- gain **Minor Strain**
- gain a condition (Chilled, Weighed, Blighted—per your condition appendix later)
- advance a domain overlay track

---

## 5) Supply model (simple, works)
Use **Supply Units** (SU). Start with 6–12 SU depending on campaign.
- Each watch of travel costs **1 SU** (food/water/fuel).
- Severe domains may cost **+1 SU** per watch (Kaldraun/Khalsarra/Charnelix).
- Successful Quartermaster checks can recover or preserve SU.

**When SU hits 0:** each watch costs Minor Strain and forces a Con save (DC 13) or gain a condition appropriate to the domain.

---

## 6) Domain overlays in exploration (when to tick)
Overlay ticks happen when:
- you fail an obstacle in that domain
- you choose Fast pace in a harsh domain
- you camp unsheltered in a harsh domain
- you trigger a domain tell (spore bloom, whiteout line, oathstone hum)

**Default cadence:** 0–1 ticks per watch, 1 tick on a hard failure, never more than 2 without a major event.

Track 2/4/6 events are from your Domain Overlay rules (or from a monster tied to that domain).

---

## 7) Encounter checks (keep it light)
Per watch (overland) or every 3 turns (site), roll 1d6:
- **1:** encounter
- **2:** sign/omen (foreshadow)
- **3–6:** no encounter

If the overlay is at Track 4+, treat a 2 as an encounter sign *plus* a minor complication.

---

## 8) Camps and Shelter (the “safe play” reward)
Creating a safe camp is an obstacle.
- Quartermaster/Survival to find a site
- Ritecraft to set witness lines
- Tools to build shelter

On success:
- reduce overlay by 1 tick OR prevent the next Track 2 effect this night
- recover 1 kit charge or 1 SU (GM)

On failure:
- still rest, but gain Minor Strain or lose SU (fail-forward)

---

## 9) Site exploration actions (10-minute turns)
Each turn, a PC can do 1 main action:
- Move carefully / map
- Search
- Force entry
- Treat wounds / contamination
- Set a ward / witness line
- Hide / scout ahead
- Craft quick counterplay item (mask, chalk line, firebreak)

Use the Procedure rules: success grants progress; failure grants progress with a cost.

---

## 10) Quick tables (DM tools)
### 10.1 Hazard prompts by domain
- **Kaldraun:** whiteout wall, ice-shelf crack, breath freeze, drift maze
- **Khalsarra:** fault collapse, burden fog, vow-echo trap, sinkstone
- **Charnelix:** spore cloud, leech mire, contamination web, false solid ground
- **Varook:** hush corridor, memory loop path, unseen watchers, truth-snag
- **Shakora:** flood law checkpoint, river pull, treaty toll, undertow
- **Keairvari:** cliff wind shear, storm front, narrow pass, rockfall
- **Nyvakira:** emberfall, smoke pocket, forge-heat, ash slide
- **Gaianok:** living bramble maze, mutualist ward test, animal omen, root bridge

### 10.2 Exploration rewards (non-combat)
- maps, safe routes, witness tokens
- crafting materials (region materials list)
- favors and tags (Trusted, Owed)
- relic clues (Pet‑Marked paths)

---

## 11) Minimal outputs (what to write)
Per watch/turn:
- domain + pace
- roles assigned
- 1 obstacle + 1 check
- SU change
- overlay tick change
- encounter roll result

Keep it tight. Keep moving.
