# Calanthor Equipment: Tiers, Crafting, and Starting Loadouts (v1.0)
*Adapts weapons/implements to how Calanthor actually works: Great Pets, harsh regions, rites, and treaty logistics.*

This module sits on top of **Calanthor Weapons & Implements** and answers:
- What counts as “common” vs “ritebound” in Calanthor?
- When do resonance hooks appear?
- How do characters start equipped by culture/background?
- How does crafting work using Procedure Checks (fail-forward)?

---

## 1) Calanthor item philosophy (official)
Calanthor is not a “+1 weapon economy.” Power comes from:
- **techniques**, **class edges**, and **good play**
- **counterplay** and **ritual preparation**
- **region pressure** and **overlay track**

So items should:
- be *identity-forward* (culture, dominion, rite marks)
- give **utility hooks** (procedure/counterplay/communication)
- avoid flat combat bonuses

---

## 2) Equipment Tiers (Calanthor-native)
These tiers are **immediately usable** at the table and generator-friendly.

### Tier 0 — Trade Steel (Common)
**What it is:** mass-traded blades, bows, and tools.  
**Rules:** no resonance hooks. Normal 5e baseline.  
**Where it comes from:** treaty markets, border forts, caravan stock.

### Tier 1 — Clanwork (Crafted)
**What it is:** culturally-made gear (cairn stamps, ash-braids, tide knots).  
**Rules:** may carry **tags** and **one “tool truth”** (non-mechanical or very narrow).  
**Allowed benefit:** advantage on **one** *non-combat* Procedure Check **1/scene** *only if* it makes sense and doesn’t violate the One-Modifier Rule.

### Tier 2 — Ritebound (Rite-Stamped)
**What it is:** gear that has been **named** by a rite (witnessed, etched, corded, sung).  
**Rules:** may have **one Resonance Hook** from the weapon/implement doc:
- passive narrative utility *or*
- 1/scene or 1/short rest **counterplay/procedure** utility  
**No combat math buffs.**

### Tier 3 — Pet‑Marked (Dominion Relic)
**What it is:** a Great Pet’s dominion has **left a mark** (light or dark).  
**Rules:** may have **two** hooks *or* one stronger hook (still non-damage).  
**Always has:** `SPIRIT:LIGHT` or `SPIRIT:DARK` tag.  
**Acquisition:** story-earned only (quest, bargain, rite of rebinding, dominion trial).

> **Hard rule:** Even Tier 3 items do not grant +to hit or +damage by default.  
> If you want “+1” items later, make them ultra-rare artifacts and keep them campaign-specific.

---

## 3) Spirit Alignment on gear (Calanthor feel)
Ritebound/Pet‑Marked items can be **Light** or **Dark** aligned.

### Using “opposed” gear
Wielding an opposed Pet‑Marked relic is not forbidden, but it brings pressure:
- social suspicion (treaty courts, rite circles)
- omens/tells (the relic “doesn’t sit right”)

**Optional mechanical pressure (recommended, light touch):**
- 1/long rest, the first time you invoke an opposed relic’s hook, you gain **Minor Strain**.

This keeps the lore sharp without punishing players constantly.

## 4) Crafting in Calanthor (Procedure Check driven)
Crafting is handled as an **Obstacle** using your Procedure Check rules.
Crafting should *move forward* even on bad rolls.

### 4.1 What you need to craft
- **Base profile:** choose the weapon/implement baseline
- **Workspace:** forge, campwork, rite circle, or field kit
- **Material:** at least one region material (below) for Clanwork+
- **Rite authority (Tier 2+):** a witness, circle, or acceptable rite practice

### 4.2 Craft DCs and time (default)
| Tier | DC | Time | Cost |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Trade Steel | 10 | 1 hour | cheap materials |
| Clanwork | 12 | 4 hours | standard materials |
| Ritebound | 15 | 1 day | rare materials + witness |
| Pet‑Marked | 18 | 3 days | relic material + dominion trial |

**Checks:** choose the most fitting:
- **Smithing/Tools** (gear, forging, fitting)
- **Ritecraft** (naming, binding, witness lines)
- **Nature/Medicine** (verdant binders, antidotes)
- **Survival** (field building, tundra assembly)

### 4.3 Fail-forward outcomes (use Procedure rules)
On a **failure**, pick one (or roll):
- **Time cost:** +50% time
- **Resource cost:** consume an extra material
- **Flaw tag:** gains a tell or drawback (loud, brittle, omen-bright)
- **Debt:** you owe a witness/circle/faction
- **Pressure:** overlay track ticks +1 during the next relevant obstacle

On a **success with cost**, apply a smaller version of the above.

## 5) Region Materials (what Calanthor gear is “made of”)
Use these to justify tags and hooks.

### Kaldraun (Frozen Tundra)
- **Driftglass** (hard ice-crystal) — stealth/whiteout utility

### Khalsarra (Cracked Earth)
- **Oathstone** (dense basalt marked by vows) — anchor/weight rites

### Charnelix (Sable Mire)

### Varook (Gloomwood Border)

### Shakora (Waters)
- **Tide-salt** — cleansing, binding knots, law seals
- **Riverstone ink** — treaties, witness lines, parley tech

### Keairvari (Skyward Peaks)
- **Windcord** — signaling, navigation, updraft gear
- **Storm-scribed brass** — omen tools, watch gear

### Nyvakira (Emberwood)
- **Embersteel** — heat control, safe ignition gear
- **Chain-rhythm links** — strain venting, tempo rites

## 6) Starting Loadouts by Culture (recommended defaults)
These are **starting options**, not restrictions. If a player wants a different weapon profile, reskin it.

Each loadout gives:
- 1 weapon (Trade Steel or Clanwork)
- 1 implement/tool
- 1 signature “flavor kit” (supports counterplay/procedure)

### Frostborn (Kaldraun-adjacent)
- Weapon: **Cairn-Hammer** *(Clanwork Warhammer)* or Spear
- Implement: **Rime Beads**
- Kit: cold-weather gear + rope + pitons + chalk

### Ashwalker (Khalsarra routes)
- Weapon: **Oathstone Maul** *(Ritebound by default at level 3+, otherwise Clanwork)* or Pike
- Implement: **Rite Chalk & Cord**
- Kit: ash pouch + prybar + water ration

### Shakoran (Waters)
- Weapon: **Tide Trident** *(Clanwork)* or Scimitar
- Implement: **Water Sash**
- Kit: law seal token + clean water + net/rope

### Gaianok Steward (Verdant Valley)
- Weapon: **Bramble Spear** *(Clanwork)* or Quarterstaff
- Implement: **Seed Pouch & Shears**
- Kit: poultice pack + cloth masks + salting jar

### Keairvari Sentinel (Skyward Peaks)
- Weapon: **Windglaive** *(Clanwork)* or Longbow
- Implement: **Wind Chimes Harness**
- Kit: signal flags + pitons + weather notes

### Nyvakira Rite‑Dancer (Emberwood)
- Weapon: **Emberchain** *(Clanwork Flail)* or Shortsword
- Implement: **Ember Rod**
- Kit: tinder + heat wraps + ash cloth

### Mire Marshrunner (Charnelix border)
- Weapon: **Sporehook Sickle** *(Clanwork)* or Net
- Implement: **Seed Pouch & Shears** (verdant countercraft) or Water Sash (cleanse)
- Kit: respirator mask + salt + burning oil

### Gloomward Witness (Varook border)
- Weapon: **Namecord Whip** *(Clanwork)* or Rapier
- Implement: **Rite Chalk & Cord** or **Echo-slate** (if you add it later)
- Kit: name tokens + candle + ink

## 7) Crafting progression (how hooks enter play)
To keep balance consistent:
- **Levels 1–2:** mostly Trade Steel / Clanwork (hooks are rare)
- **Levels 3–6:** Ritebound becomes available (1 hook)
- **Levels 7+:** Pet‑Marked relics become reasonable rewards (story items)

If your campaign is grittier, push these up by 2 levels.

---

## 8) GM quick adjudication (2 sentences)
If a player asks “does my gear help here?”:
1) If the gear’s tag/hook is relevant, allow **one** advantage or +1 effect per scene (One-Modifier Rule).  
2) If they’re trying to turn gear into a damage buff, redirect to techniques, tactics, or story rewards.
