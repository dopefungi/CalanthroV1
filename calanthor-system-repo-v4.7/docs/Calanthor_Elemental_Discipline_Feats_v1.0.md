# Calanthor Feat Blocks — Elemental Disciplines (v1.0)
*Official 5e-style feat writeups for learning Calanthor Techniques.*

These feats implement the Technique Library’s recommended learning path:
- Initiate → Adept → Master
- Techniques are **non-spell** abilities (actions/reactions/rites) with standardized DCs.
- Designed to add “bending complexity” without replacing spellcasting or martial identity.

---

## Shared rules for these feats (official)
### Technique Focus Ability
When you gain a Discipline via these feats, choose your **Technique Focus Ability** for that discipline from:
- **FIRE:** CHA or CON
- **AIR:** DEX or WIS
- **WATER:** WIS or CHA
- **ICE:** CON or WIS
- **EARTH:** STR or CON
- **VERDANT:** WIS

You use this ability for:
- **Technique Save DC = 8 + PB + Focus mod**
- **Technique Attack = PB + Focus mod**

### Technique learning and swapping
- When you learn a technique, choose from the **Calanthor Technique Library v1.0**.
- Whenever you gain a level, you may replace **one** technique you know with another from a discipline you have access to (GM may require a short training montage or teacher).
- You cannot learn a technique of a tier you don’t qualify for (Novice/Adept/Master), as defined below.

### Tier access by character level (recommended default)
- **Novice techniques:** any level
- **Adept techniques:** level **5+**
- **Master techniques:** level **11+**

(If your table prefers stricter pacing, the GM may require Adept feats for Adept techniques and Master feats for Master techniques even at the appropriate level.)

### Stance rule reminder
If a technique is marked **STANCE**, you can have only **one** stance active at a time.

## FEAT: Elemental Discipline Initiate
**Prerequisite:** None

You awaken training in an elemental discipline and learn to shape it as Techniques.

Choose one discipline: **FIRE, AIR, WATER, ICE, EARTH, or VERDANT**.
You gain the following benefits:

- **Technique Focus:** Choose your Focus Ability for the chosen discipline (see shared rules).
- **Novice Techniques:** Learn **two Novice techniques** from that discipline.
- **Discipline Tag:** You gain the discipline’s tag for generator/lore interactions (region resonance, overlay texture, etc.).

**Special:** If you already have techniques from another source (class/subclass/background), this feat still grants the Focus Ability choice and techniques as normal.

---

## FEAT: Elemental Discipline Adept
**Prerequisite:** Level 5+, Elemental Discipline Initiate

Your control deepens and becomes more tactical.

Choose one:
- Learn **two additional techniques** from disciplines you have via Initiate/Adept/Master (Novice or Adept), OR
- Learn **one Adept technique** and **one Novice technique** and increase your Technique Focus Ability mod used for techniques by **+1** (maximum 20 for that ability score).

**Note:** You can’t take the +1 option more than once total (across all disciplines).

---

## FEAT: Elemental Discipline Master
**Prerequisite:** Level 11+, Elemental Discipline Adept

You can shape your discipline with mythic precision.

Choose one:
- Learn **two additional techniques** (Adept or Master) from disciplines you have access to, OR
- Learn **one Master technique** and gain **Mastery Reserve**: once per long rest, when you would expend a technique use, you may ignore that expenditure.

---

## Optional feat (balanced): Dual Discipline Initiate
*(Only use if your campaign wants more hybrid benders; safe but increases complexity.)*

**Prerequisite:** Elemental Discipline Initiate

Choose a **second** discipline and gain:
- a Focus Ability for that discipline, and
- learn **one Novice technique** from that discipline.

You cannot choose this feat more than once.

---

## Druid-friendly optional feat (balanced): Verdant Keeper
**Prerequisite:** Druid level 4+ or Elemental Discipline Initiate (VERDANT)

You deepen your bond with living systems.
- Learn **one additional Novice VERDANT technique**.
- When you use a VERDANT technique to remove **Minor Strain**, you can also grant the target advantage on its next CON save vs poison/disease before the end of the scene.

This feat is optional; it exists to support druid fantasy without out-healing clerics.

---

## Integration notes (important)
- These feats add **options**, not raw combat math.
- They pair with Calanthor’s “One-Modifier Rule” cleanly (techniques are effects, not roll bonuses).
- If a technique would trivialize an obstacle, the GM should lean on:
  - Aerial Strain (for flight)
  - hazard DCs (for storms/whiteouts/spores)
  - overlay rules (tick caps, counterplay, events at track 6)
