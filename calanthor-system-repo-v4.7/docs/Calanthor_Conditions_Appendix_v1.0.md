# Calanthor Conditions Appendix (v1.0)
*A small, consistent condition set that supports Calanthor regions and overlays without bloating 5e.*

This appendix:
- keeps standard 5e conditions as-is
- defines a few **Calanthor-specific conditions**
- standardizes how overlays and hazards apply them

---

## 0) Use standard 5e conditions first
Use core 5e conditions whenever they fit (frightened, poisoned, restrained, prone, etc.).

Calanthor adds only what 5e doesn’t cover cleanly:
- Minor Strain (and optional Major Strain)
- Silenced (verbal disruption)
- Blighted (healing interference / contamination)
- Haunted (memory pressure)
- Chilled (cold performance hit)
- Weighed (despair/weight slow)

---

## 1) Minor Strain (Calanthor)
**Type:** setting condition (lightweight)

**What it represents:** exposure, fatigue, numbness, brittle breath, coughing spores, dread, overload.

**Rule:**
- While you have **Minor Strain**, take **-1** on your next d20 roll that meaningfully relates to the cause (GM decides).
- Clears on a **short rest**, or earlier if fictionally addressed (warmth, witness, ritual, remedy).

**Stacking:**
- If you gain Minor Strain while you already have it, the GM may:
  - apply Minor Strain again (two separate -1 triggers), OR
  - upgrade to **Major Strain** (optional; see below).

---

## 2) Major Strain (optional)
**Type:** setting condition (heavier)

**Rule (simple):**
- While you have **Major Strain**, you have disadvantage on **one category** chosen when gained:
  - Physical (STR/DEX-based checks)
  - Mental (INT/WIS-based checks)
  - Social (CHA-based checks)
- Clears on a **long rest** in safe shelter, or via a meaningful ritual/remedy.

Use this only if your table wants harsher endurance play.

---

## 3) Silenced
**Type:** Calanthor condition

**Rule:**
- While **Silenced**, you cannot speak above a whisper.
- You cannot cast spells that require **verbal components**.
- You have disadvantage on checks that require clear speech (Persuasion/Deception/Performance) unless the table agrees whispers suffice.

**Duration defaults:**
- until end of next turn (combat), OR
- until leaving a hush zone / finishing a rite (exploration)

**Common sources:** Varook hush pockets, Gloomwood predators, certain Keairvari wind rites (rare).

---

## 4) Blighted
**Type:** Calanthor condition

**What it represents:** contamination, infection pressure, healing resistance, rot in supplies.

**Rule (simple, non-stacking):**
- While **Blighted**, whenever you regain HP, reduce the healing by **PB** (minimum 1).
- At the end of a short rest, make a **DC 15 CON save**:
  - on success: Blighted ends
  - on failure: Blighted persists and you gain Minor Strain

**Stronger version (Deep Mire / track 4+):**
- Healing reduction is **2×PB**, and the save is DC 17.

**Common sources:** Charnelix spores, blightwells, contaminated wounds.

---

## 5) Haunted
**Type:** Calanthor condition

**What it represents:** memory pressure, intrusive echoes, psychological warfare.

**Rule:**
- While **Haunted**, you have disadvantage on **Insight** checks and **concentration checks**.
- When you fail a Wisdom save, the GM may ask for a brief “echo” complication (flashback, wrong name, doubt).

**Duration defaults:**
- 10 minutes (exploration), OR
- until you complete a witness/anchor act (see counterplay)

**Counterplay:**
- A **witness rest** (ally repeats your name/purpose) ends Haunted.
- Recording an anchor (written name/purpose) can prevent Haunted once per day.

**Common sources:** Varook domains, deep hush rites, echo glades.

---

## 6) Chilled
**Type:** Calanthor condition

**What it represents:** numb fingers, brittle breath, slowed reflexes from cold.

**Rule:**
- While **Chilled**, your speed is reduced by **5 ft**, and you have disadvantage on your next **Athletics** or **Sleight of Hand** check.
- Chilled ends when you gain warmth (shelter, fire, hot drink) or after a short rest in shelter.

**Common sources:** Kaldraun exposure, veilstorm pulses, ice hazards.

---

## 7) Weighed
**Type:** Calanthor condition

**What it represents:** despair pressure, sacred weight, emotional gravity.

**Rule:**
- While **Weighed**, your speed is reduced by **5 ft**.
- The next time you make a check to **inspire, rally, or persuade toward hope**, take **-1** (Minor Strain can represent this if you prefer fewer conditions).

**Duration defaults:**
- until you complete a purposeful action (aid an ally, uphold a vow, reach a declared waypoint), OR
- short rest with a chant circle.

**Common sources:** Khalsarra monolith fields, vow-break moments, Endless Maw proximity.

---

## 8) Standardizing “zones” and “hazards”
When a hazard creates a zone (hush pocket, spore bloom, whiteout):
- entering the zone may force a save/check
- failing applies one condition
- success may still cost time or supplies

Default DCs:
- **15** standard hazard
- **20** hostile subzone / severe conditions
- **25** mythic pressure / Great Pet attention

---

## 9) Minimal condition mapping (for generators)
If you need to generate from tags:
- FIRE → Minor Strain (scorch), frightened (predator), exhaustion (rare)
- AIR → prone (gust), restrained (wind pin), Minor Strain (breathless)
- WATER → prone (slick), restrained (undertow), Minor Strain (cold wet)
- ICE → Chilled, restrained (ice grip), Minor Strain (brittle)
- EARTH → prone, restrained, Weighed
- VERDANT → poisoned (pollen), restrained (vines), Blighted (spores)
- VAROOK_SILENCE → Silenced, Haunted, frightened
- CHARNELIX_BLIGHT → Blighted, poisoned, Minor Strain

Keep this mapping as suggestion, not a hard rule.
