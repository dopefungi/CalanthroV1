# Calanthor Domain Overlay Operating Rules (v1.0)
*How Great Pet domain pressures activate, tick, and resolve—without turning bad luck into stalled play.*

Domain Overlays are Calanthor’s “mythic pressure” layer: the realm asserting itself through heat, storms, tides, bloom, sacred weight, stasis, silence, or blight.

This document defines:
- **when** an overlay is active (and when it is not)
- **how** its track advances
- **how** players can push back
- **safety rules** that keep pacing fun even on low-roll streaks

---

## 1) What an Overlay is (and isn’t)
### Overlay = pressure dial
An overlay is a **track (0–6)** plus:
- triggers (what raises it)
- threshold effects (what happens at 2/4/6)
- counterplay (how to reduce/avoid it)
- optional monster graft traits (how it changes encounters)

### Overlay is NOT “always on”
Overlays are **OFF by default**.
- Regions and ecoregions already provide hazards and encounter identity.
- Overlays are used when you want the place to feel **awake, sacred, or dangerous**.

---

## 2) Activation states (the GM chooses one)
When you activate an overlay, choose one state. This prevents “accidental permanent pressure.”

### State A — Dormant (default)
- Overlay is **inactive**
- Track does not tick
- Threshold effects do not apply

### State B — Background Pressure (light touch)
Use for: long travel tone, looming threat, “the realm watches.”
- Track starts at **0–1**
- Track can increase **at most once per obstacle**
- Use overlay consequences only on **meaningful failures**

### State C — Active Domain (setpiece mode)
Use for: sacred sites, deep subzones, disasters, trials, Great Pet attention.
- Track starts at **1–2** (or per subzone auto-start)
- Track can increase **once per obstacle** (see safety rule), unless Cataclysm
- Threshold effects at 2/4/6 are fully in play

### State D — Cataclysm (rare story beat)
Use for: realm breach, Great Pet confrontation, campaign climax.
- Track starts at **4+**
- Track may increase more than once per obstacle if fiction demands
- Must include a clear “escape clause” (ritual, bargain, retreat, victory condition)

**Design note:** Cataclysm is drama, not attrition. It should change the situation, not lock the campaign.

---

## 3) Scope defaults (what the overlay affects)
To keep tracking manageable, overlays use a default scope.

### Default: Scene-wide
**Scene-wide** is the standard scope:
- applies to the current obstacle, encounter, or setpiece
- ends when the scene ends (unless the GM explicitly continues it)

### Travel overlay (optional)
For overland travel arcs, you may run the overlay **party-wide for a travel leg**:
- one watch (≈4 hours) or one meaningful terrain shift
- ticks at most once per leg

### Individual scope (rare)
Only use individual overlay marks as a **story consequence** (scarring, a bargain, contamination).
The overlay track remains scene-wide or travel-wide; individual effects are a narrative tag or condition.

---

## 4) Where overlays can appear
### Local by default
Each overlay belongs primarily to its Great Pet’s domain:
- Nyvakira ↔ Emberwood
- Keairvari ↔ Skyward Peaks
- Shakora ↔ Waters
- Gaianok ↔ Verdant Valley
- Khalsarra ↔ Cracked Earth
- Kaldraun ↔ Frozen Tundra
- Varook ↔ Gloomwood
- Charnelix ↔ Sable Mire

### Intrusions (explicit flag)
An overlay may appear outside its domain only if the GM declares an **Intrusion Event**:
- a rift/breach
- antagonist ritual
- artifact contamination
- Great Pet rivalry spillover

**Rule:** if you didn’t declare intrusion, keep overlays local. This prevents “pressure soup.”

---

## 5) The tick rule (how the track increases)
Overlays advance when the overlay is active (Background/Active/Cataclysm).

### Safety rule (official)
**An overlay track can increase at most once per obstacle** (unless Cataclysm).

This is the core anti-spiral rule: multiple bad rolls do not automatically stack multiple ticks.

### What counts as an “obstacle”
An obstacle is one meaningful barrier beat:
- a stormfront
- crossing a ravine
- a purification rite
- negotiating passage/treaty
- one watch of travel in a hostile subzone

Do not treat “each roll” as a new obstacle.

### Standard tick triggers
When an overlay is active, increase its track by **+1** if any of the following occur (GM choice):
1) A relevant **Procedure Check fails**, and you select “Domain Pressure +1” as the consequence.
2) A relevant check **fails by 5+** (optional margin rule): the overlay tick is an appropriate major consequence.
3) The party **ignores a visible counterplay** that was clearly offered (refuse shelter, refuse rite, refuse detour).
4) The party commits a clear **domain violation/taboo** in a charged place (pollute sacred water, break a vow at monoliths, speak taboo names in a hush pocket, etc.).

### Success interaction (keeps pressure fair)
When a relevant check **succeeds by 5+**, the GM may reduce the overlay track by **-1** if it makes sense:
- you secured shelter well
- you performed the rite flawlessly
- you navigated the hazard cleanly
- you made restitution convincingly

---

## 6) What happens at 2 / 4 / 6 (universal philosophy)
Each overlay defines its own threshold effects. Use this philosophy to keep them fun:

- **2:** warning & friction (Minor Strain, small speed penalty, a brief disadvantage once)
- **4:** meaningful pressure (reaction loss, healing reduction, forced choice, visibility loss)
- **6:** **event**, not “game over” (storm breaks, blight bloom, despair spiral, hush bargain)

**Official rule:** Track 6 creates a dramatic situation change that offers options:
- retreat
- bargain
- ritual
- fight
- sacrifice a resource to stabilize

No “you can’t play” locks.

---

## 7) Counterplay (how players push back)
Every overlay scene should surface at least **one visible counterplay** option.

Counterplay categories (aim for at least 3 total per overlay):
1) **Resource**: spend supplies, spell slots, antitoxin, shelter kit
2) **Action**: Procedure Check, ritual, navigation, mediation
3) **Choice**: detour, vow, bargain, accept a boundary

### Default recovery (recommended)
- Proper **short rest shelter** during an active overlay: reduce track by **-1** (or prevent one tick)
- Proper **long rest in safe shelter**: overlay ends; travel overlays reset to **0**

(Your individual overlay files may define stronger/weaker recovery—this is the default.)

---

## 8) Bad-luck protection (official overlay safety package)
These rules ensure overlays don’t punish unlucky streaks.

### A) Assured Progress (mandatory for overlays)
If the party fails the same obstacle twice, the third attempt cannot be a dead end:
- convert to **success with cost**, or
- lower DC by 5, or
- grant advantage

### B) Complication cap (recommended)
Within a single obstacle, allow **one major complication** from overlay ticking.
Additional failures become **minor complications** (time, small supplies, flavor strain).

### C) Help Always Helps (recommended)
Meaningful help grants **advantage** or **+2** (table choice).
Teamwork smooths variance and keeps travel fun.

---

## 9) Quick activation triggers (GM checklist)
Turn an overlay to Background or Active when **any** of these are true:

### Location triggers
- deep subzone (Deep Gloom / Deep Mire)
- sacred site (monolith field, Shardspire, river oath-stone)
- signature phenomenon (veilstorm, blightwell, hush pocket)

### Behavior triggers
- taboo/violation
- vow-break
- exploitation without restitution

### Story triggers
- intrusion event
- Great Pet attention/omen
- antagonist ritual “turning the dial”

---

## 10) Examples (how it looks at the table)
### Frozen Tundra (Kaldraun — Frost Lock)
- State: Background during travel, Active at veilstorm
- Tick: once per watch if shelter fails; or on damage while exposed
- Counterplay: cairn rite, shelter, controlled fire, shared watch rotation

### Cracked Earth (Khalsarra — Sacred Weight)
- State: Active near monoliths / Endless Maw
- Tick: aimless travel, vow-break at grief-sites, refusing mercy
- Counterplay: declare purpose, chant circle, witnessed vow, compassion act

### Waters (Shakora — Tide Owed)
- State: Active during treaty negotiation at sacred ford
- Tick: pollution, broken “river promise,” refusal to mediate
- Counterplay: restitution, cleansing rite, mediated settlement

---

## Recommended default configuration (best feel)
- Overlays are **Dormant by default**.
- Use **Scene-wide** scope unless running a travel leg.
- Enforce “tick once per obstacle” (unless Cataclysm).
- Use Assured Progress for overlay obstacles.
