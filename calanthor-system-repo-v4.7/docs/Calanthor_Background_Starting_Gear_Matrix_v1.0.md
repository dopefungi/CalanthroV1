# Background Starting Gear Matrix (v1.0)
*Plug‑and‑play equipment picks that reinforce class identity, culture ties, and Calanthor play patterns.*

Use this at character creation:
1) pick **Background** (Calanthor-flavored)
2) choose 1 option from Weapon/Implement lists
3) take the kit
4) if your table uses Culture as separate, the GM can swap the background label to match the PC’s homeland

**Tier guidance:** Starting gear is usually **Trade Steel** or **Clanwork**.  
Ritebound gear appears as rewards or level 3+ starts (GM choice).

---

## Backgrounds (Calanthor-native)
### 1) Frostborn Guide (Kaldraun edge)
- **Weapon (choose 1):** Spear, Warhammer, Dagger  
- **Implement (choose 1):** Rime Beads, Rite Chalk & Cord  
- **Kit:** cold-weather gear, rope, pitons, chalk, tinder

### 2) Ashwalker Caravan Guard (Khalsarra routes)
- **Weapon:** Pike, Maul, Battleaxe  
- **Implement:** Rite Chalk & Cord, Stone Gauntlets  
- **Kit:** prybar, ash pouch, water ration, spare wraps

### 3) Mire Marshrunner (Charnelix border)
- **Weapon:** Spear, Shortsword, Sickle *(region add-on)*  
- **Implement:** Seed Pouch & Shears, Water Sash  
- **Kit:** respirator mask, salt, oil, antitoxin (1)

### 4) Gloomward Witness (Varook border)
- **Weapon:** Rapier, Whip *(region add-on)*, Dagger  
- **Implement:** Rite Chalk & Cord, (optional) Echo-slate if available  
- **Kit:** ink, name tokens, candle, cord, hooded lantern

### 5) Shakoran River‑Law Envoy (Waters)
- **Weapon:** Scimitar, Shortsword, Trident *(region add-on)*  
- **Implement:** Water Sash, Rite Chalk & Cord  
- **Kit:** law seal token, clean water, travel papers, net/rope *(optional)*

### 6) Keairvari Peak Courier (Skyward Peaks)
- **Weapon:** Glaive, Longbow, Shortsword  
- **Implement:** Wind Chimes Harness  
- **Kit:** signal flags, pitons, weather notes, climbing harness

### 7) Nyvakira Ember Artisan (Emberwood)
- **Weapon:** Warhammer, Longsword, Whip *(region add-on)*

### 8) Gaianok Steward‑Warden (Verdant Valley)
- **Weapon:** Spear, Quarterstaff, Sickle *(region add-on)*

### 9) Treaty City Duelist (cosmopolitan)
- **Weapon:** Rapier, Longsword, Hand Crossbow  
- **Implement:** Rite Chalk & Cord (duel rites)  
- **Kit:** fine clothes, signet, ink, 50 ft silk rope

### 10) Border Fort Skirmisher (any harsh edge)
- **Weapon:** Pike, Longbow, Light Crossbow  
- **Implement:** Stone Gauntlets or Wind Chimes Harness (pick based on region)  
- **Kit:** field rations, spare bowstring, pitons, torch, manacles

## Optional: “1 hook start” (GM switch)
If you want a slightly higher‑magic start:
- one PC may begin with **Tier 2 Ritebound** gear (1 resonance hook),
- but the GM should attach a **Debt** (circle/faction/witness) or a **Flaw tag**.
